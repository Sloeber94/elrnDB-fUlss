Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: front seat detached, rear seat detached, front and rear door seals detached.

The procedure in question involves the complementary operation of replacing the front and rear doors and stripping the floor panel lining, the centre pillar, the rear pillar and removing the roof panel.
