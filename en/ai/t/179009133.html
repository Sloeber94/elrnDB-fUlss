Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

When reverse gear is engaged, the apporpriate command is sent by the switch I020, located on the gearbox. The switch is supplied by the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 10 connector C) through the line protected by fuse F01 located in the actual junction unit. When reverse gear is engaged, the switch I020 closes its internal contact, sending the signal to the body computer node M001 (pin 5 connector B).

On versions with automatic transmission, there is no reversing switch on the gearbox, but this function is carried out by a special contact "R" for the automatic transmission solenoid valve unit L050

Having received the command, the body computer node M001 closes the contact inside the relay and supplies the parking sensor control unit M084 (pin 1 connector A) via the line protected by fuse F24 (pin 3 connector H); control unit M084 is earthed at pin 7 connector A.

The control unit M084 operates the speaker P044, used as an acoustic warning for signalling that reverse gear is engaged and that the parking sensors are activated, from pins 2, 10.

The parking control unit M084 receives information, from connector B, from the parking sensor unit K097, located in the rear bumper, which measures the distance between the vehicle and any obstacles.

A system inhibition signal reaches pin 12 if a trailer is connected via coupling D73.

Connector D73 also receives a supply from the body computer node M001 via the line protected by fuse F10.
