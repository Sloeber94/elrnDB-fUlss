Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

This device facilitates the parking stage: when reverse is engaged, it indicates the approach of an obstacle that could be outsid the driver's visual range.

The system consists of four ultrasound sensors located inside the bumpers that send given signals to a specific control unit: once processed, the control unit is able to calculate the distance from possible obstacles and communicate it by means of a speaker, emitting an intermittent sound signal at a frequency proportional to the distance from the detected obstacle.

When the calculated distance is less than 25 [cm], the sound emitted becomes acute and constant.

If the distance of the obstacle detected increases, or if the driver disengages reverse gear, the sound stops. The control unit is activated with reverse gear engaged:

If the distance of the obstacle detected remains constant, the sound stops after about 3 seconds.

The signal that reverse gear is engaged is managed by the body computer node which sends this information to the parking sensor control unit via the CAN.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

The operation of the parking sensors is automatically deactivated when the trailer is electrically connected. The sensors are automatically reactivated when the trailer is disconnected.

See E2050 TRAILER WIRING
