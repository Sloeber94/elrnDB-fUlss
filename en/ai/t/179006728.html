The fan that sends air into the passenger compartment is not working.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The networks go on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
