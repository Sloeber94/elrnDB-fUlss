Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Remove the steering box heat shield.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the exhaust pipe (1b) and keep the metal gasket (1c).

2. Undo the fixing nut (1a) and, using a special extractor (1b), remove the steering track rod end (1c) from its housing.

Then move the exhaust pipe out of the working area.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
