Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The electric adjustment of the front seats is operated by specific switches (H066 and H067) and controlled by specific relays (J139 and J140) also housed in the seats.

The Body computer node M001 supplies (pin 3 connector F) the coils for relays J139 and J140 (pin 1).

The power network consists of two lines protected by fuses F32 and F33 located inside the additional fuse box B098 (pins G32 and G33 connector B respectively) which supply the relays J139 and J140 (pin 3) respectively.
