1. Remove the lower bolt.

2. Insert a suitable tool in the bolt housing and extract the upper pin.

3. Remove the ignition switch complete with electrical wiring.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

- Place the ignition switch in its housing in the steering column.

- Insert the upper pin.

- Tighten a new bolt.

- Connect the electrical connections.

- Secure the wiring suitably.
