Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The heated rear window operation command is supplied by a switch in climate control unit M070 (pins 10, 14, 15 connector A). The command is sent to body computer node M001 (pins 6, 8, 10 connector D respectively) via a CAN line.

The control unit M070 (pins 1 and 2 connector A) also communicates the information that the heated rear windscreen is activated to the instrument panel E050 (pins 5 and 9) and from there to the various displays.

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

The body computer node M001 simultaneously supplies:

- from pins 1 and 9 of connector H, heated rear window P055 at pin 1 of connector A;

- from pin 9 of connector F, driver side door control unit M066 and passenger side door control unit M067, both at pin 1 of connector A.

The command is supplied by body computer node M001 (pins 36, 38 connector I, for the driver side and pin 38, 40 connector L, for the passenger side) to door control units M066 (pins 20, 19 connector A) and M067 (pins 19,20 connector A) via the CAN line.

Control units M066 and M067 (pins 4, 5 connector B) also supply the following: internal heater coil of left door mirror P060 (pin 4, 5 connector B) and right door mirror P061 (pins 4, 5 connector B).
