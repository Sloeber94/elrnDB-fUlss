Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The Body computer node M001 supplies the driver's door control unit M066 and the passenger door control unit M067, both at pin 1 of connector A from pin 9 of connector F.

The command is supplied from the Body computer node M001 (pins 36, 38 connector I for the driver's side and pin 38, 40 connector L for the passenger side) to the door control units M066 and M067 (pins 19, 20 connector A) via the CAN.

Information on the current position of the windows and the opening/closing commands through the operation of the remote control is provided via the CAN.

The door control unit M066 and the switch unit H051 interact to carry out a series of operations:

- unit H051 is supplied at pin 2 of connector A from pin 16 of connector A at M066;

- the earth signal at pin 5 of the same connector at H051 is supplied from pin 17 of connector A at M066;

- the activating signal for the windows is sent from pins 4 and 6 of connector A of unit H051 to pins 3 and 14 of the same connector of control unit M066.

The opening/closing command for the passenger window also takes place through switch H050 which interacts with the door control unit M067:

- H050 is supplied at pin 2 from pin 16 of connector A at M067;

- the windows activating signal is sent from pins 3 and 6 of H050 to pins 7 and 2 of connector A respectively of control unit M067.
