Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst audio CDs are playing there are problems with the musical reproduction (audio interruptions, skipping one or more tracks, repeating the current track).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Given the nature of the CD mechanism and the reading technique, it is absolutely normal that one or more of the above conditions could occur with the CD player, if the vehicle/CONNECT system is subject to strong impacts or stresses that affect the dashboard or driving over potholes or humps at speed. In addition, there cuold be considerable differences between the reproduction or original (pressed) CDs and master copies because the tests are always carried out using original compact discs. Also bear in mind that the introduction of methods to prevent copying can, at times, have effects on the reproduction of the disc.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

All references to R/R or repair operations and wiring diagrams may be found in the Workshop Manual.
