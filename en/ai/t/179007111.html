Removing

Refitting

Op. 5010B18 CLIMATE CONTROL UNIT AIR INTAKE - R.R.

See in Op. 1048A10

1. Remove the air hose from the intercooler to the intake manifold.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and disconnect the starter motor supply leads (1b).

2. Undo the retaining bolts (2a) and remove starter motor (2b) from the top part of the engine compartment.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the pipe from the separator (1a) and from the attachment on the cylinder head (1b).

2. Disconnect the vapour pipe from the crankcase.

3. Remove the oil vapour pipe complete with engine oil filler.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the oil vapour pipe complete with engine oil filler.

- Connect the vapour pipe to the crankcase.

- Connect the pipe to the separator and to the attachment on the cylinder head.

- Fit the starter motor and tighten the retaining bolts to torque.

- Connect the starter motor electrical wiring and tighten the fixing nuts.

- Fit the air hose from the intercooler to the intake manifold.

See in Op. 1048A10
