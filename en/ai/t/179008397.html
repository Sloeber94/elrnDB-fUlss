Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The cooling system for the engine radiator and air conditioning condenser consists of two fans operated at different speeds by means of a connection between them either in series or parallel.

  • the first speed is activated at an initial engine coolant temperature level or a certain air conditioning refrigerant fluid pressure;
  • the second speed is engaged at a higher temperature or pressure.
