1040B56 FUEL RETURN LINE - R R

Path
Procedures > 10 ENGINE > 1040 FUEL TANK AND PIPES > 1040B FUEL SUPPLY/RETURN CIRCUIT PIPE > 1040B56 FUEL RETURN LINE - R R
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fUlss/en/#/179006989

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

1. Disconnect the rapid connector connected to the intermediate fuel return pipe.

- Loosen the bolts fixing the tank and lower it until the rapid connectors on the pump can be accessed.

2. Disconnect the rapid connector for the fuel return pipe on the pump.

- Remove the rear fuel return pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the rear fuel return pipe.

- Connect the rapid connector for the fuel return pipe on the pump.

- Tighten the tank fixing bolts to torque.

- Connect the rapid connector connected to the intermediate fuel return pipe.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
