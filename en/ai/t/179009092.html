Operational Description

Headlamp alignment adjustment is managed by a dedicated selector H015. Pin 2 receives a supply from the battery via fuse F12 located in the body computer node M001. This supply is only provided if the dipped headlamps are switched on, via the relay, also located in the body computer node M001.

The relay is controlled by the body computer node M001 as soon as the dipped headlamps are switched on < R1 > . Pin 3 of H015 is earthed, whilst a variable voltage signals is sent from pin 3 to pin 7 of the front light clusters F010 and F011.

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

The correction motors inside the headlamps contain a transducer that estabilishes the travel to be completed on the basis of adjustment signal voltage. The motors receive a power supply from pin 9 of the front light clusters F010 and F011 from the line protected by fuse F12 of the body computer node M001. The dipped headlamps are connected at pin 1 of the front light clusters F010 and F011.

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS
