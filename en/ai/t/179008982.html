Description

The body computer node controls the operation of the driver's and passenger side rear electric window motors.

The electric windows for the sliding rear doors are activated either by means of the switches located on the driver's front door or through those on each of the rear doors.

The electric rear windows are manually operated (the glass only moves up or down when the button is pressed).

For safety reasons, a special "locking" button, located on the driver's front door, allows the power supply to the switches on the rear doors to be cut off (in addition to opening/closing the sliding doors < R1 > and the opening rear roofs < R2 > to prevent operated, for example if children are present.

See E3095 SLIDING SIDE DOORS

The rear electric windows are also equipped with an anti-crush safety system. the control unit is capable of detecting the presence of obstacles whilst the window is closing by means of the anti-crush sensors: in this case, the system interrupts the travel of the window and reverses the direction immediately.

With the key in position S, the windows remain activated for around 1 minute.

If the remote control button is pressed in order to activate the "passenger compartment ventilation" function: the front and rear side windows open completely to allow the introduction of outside air into the passenger compartment

See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING
