Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

A standard type current socket is housed in the centre of the dashboard: the socket contains a cigar lighter resistance that can also be used to connect other instruments or devices because it works it is a 12V power point. Press the cigar lighter knob to operate the lighter: after a few seconds it will emerge automatically, ready for use.

The cigar lighter gets extremely hot. Handle it carefully and keep it out of reach of children: risk or fire or burns. Always check that the cigar lighter is switched off.

The socket is lit up when the side lights are on.

The socket is equipped with a thermal protection device: this protection cuts off the power supply if devices that absorb a lot of energy are connected.

Two further power points are present, with protective covers. Their specifications are similar to those described for the cigar lighter: one located next to the driver's seat and one at the rear next to the third row of seats.

The circuit for each current socket is protected by a specific fuse: the one for the front socket is located in the Body computer node, the other for the two rear sockets is located in the additional fuse box.
