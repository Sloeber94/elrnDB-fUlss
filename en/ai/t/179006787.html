Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6, 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Check that the fuel pump suply voltage is not less than 12V.

Measure the pump current absorption.

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Disconnect the heater plugs control unit terminal.

Disconnect the two connectors for the Webasto control unit (if fitted)

Using the Examiner in "Instrument" mode, select "Ammeter" and fit the 10A shunt between the negative battery pole and the system negative terminal.

Make sure that the courtesy lights are switched off.

Turn the ignition key to the ON position and, within 10 seconds, read the pump absorption (subtracting the consumers supplied by the ignition from the measurement) which should be between 2 and 10A.
