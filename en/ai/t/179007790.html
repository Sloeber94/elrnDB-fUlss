Removing

Refitting

Before removing and refitting the passenger Air Bag module disabling switch, the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it; (2) wait for 2 minutes before continuing; (3) remove the battery compartment cover, disconnect the negative battery terminal and insulate the leads; (4) wait for 5 minutes before continuing.

1. Release the trim using a suitable tool.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

- Remove the side trim complete with passenger Air Bag disabling switch.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Position the side trim complete with passenger Air Bag disabling switch.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Check the operation of the system using the Examiner or other diagnostic equipment.
