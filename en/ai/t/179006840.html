Removing

Refitting

Secure the power unit using suitable chains fastened to a hydraulic lift.

Position the power unit complete with supporting equipment on a support.

1. Undo the bolts securing the starter motor.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

1. Disconnect the engine rpm sensor electrical connector.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and separate the gearbox casing (1b) from the power unit.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the gearbox casing and tighten the bolts fixing it to the power unit to torque.

Connect the engine rpm sensor electrical connector.

Tighten the bolts fixing the starter motor to torque.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the fixing nuts (1a) and disconnect the starter motor electrical connections (1b).

2. Undo the retaining bolts (2a) and remove starter motor (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing nut (1a) and disconnect the earth lead on the gearbox casing (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the engine rpm sensor electrical connector.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fastenings (1a) and remove the engine flywheel guard (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and separate the gearbox casing (1b) from the power unit.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
