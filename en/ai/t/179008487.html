Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two reversing lights located in the rear light clusters.

These are operated whenever reverse is engaged via a switch on the outside of the gear housing.

The reversing lights circuit is supplied when the key is in position M.

The signal that reverse gear has been engaged is managed by the Body Computer which sends this information to the steering wheel node

  • for the automatic operation of the rearscreen wiperSee E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE
  • for the electro-chrome rear view mirrorSee E3032 DOOR MIRROR ADJUSTMENT
  • and to the parking sensors control unitSee E3090 PARKING SENSOR
