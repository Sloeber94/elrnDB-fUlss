Carry out the same operation on both sides of the vehicle.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the anti-roll bar to the suspension frame.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the front suspension frame to the bodyshell.

3. Lower the suspension frame (3a) to allow the removal of the anti-roll bar (3b) and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
