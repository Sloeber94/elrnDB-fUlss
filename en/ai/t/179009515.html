Operational Description

The centralized diagnostic socket R010 receives an ignition-controlled supply, at pin 1, through the line protected by fuse F18 of the body computer M001, pin 2 of connector B; a direct supply from the battery arrives at pin 16 via the line protected by fuse F10 of the body computer M001, pin 10 of connector B.

Pins 4 and 5 of R010 are earthed.

The diagnostic socket R010 is connected, at pin 11, to the Body Computer M001, pin 7 of connector M: through this line (K line) it is possible to connect with all the nodes connected to the network via the CAN.

Pin 12 is connected, via the K line, to the ABS control unit M050 (pin 11 of connector A) < R1 > , < R2 >

See E7023 ESP

Pin 7 is connected, via the K line, to the engine management control unit M010 < R1 > or < R2 > .

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The automatic transmission control unit M055 (pin 18) which also manages diagnostic line L, from pin 17 of M055 to pin 15 of R010, is also connected from pin 7 via the K line.

See E7050 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION

The additional heater control unit M075 (JTD only) is connected to pin 1 also from pin 7 via the K line.

See E6015 ADDITIONAL HEATER

Pin 13 is connected, via the K line, to the headlamp control units F010 and F011 (versions with gas discharge headlamps) to pin 5

See E3080 HEADLAMP AGLINMENT CORRECTOR
