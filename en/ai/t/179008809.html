Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

There are two lights for lighting up the ground ("puddle lights") in the front front doors.

These "puddle lights" come on automatically when the door is opened, irrespective of the position of the ignition key.

The courtesy light operation is timed according to a logic managed by the body computer: when one front door is opened the centre light comes on, without the spot lights and also the puddle lights; the operation is timed: a few seconds after the doors are closed, the lights go out gradually.

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS
