[Both]

Op. 7065C34 L/H OR R/H SUN BLIND (ONE) - R+R

- Remove both upper wind seals.

- Partly prise up the seals around the front doors, the side doors and the tailgate to allow the ceiling lining to be detached.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the mouldings.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the fastening brackets on the right and left centre pillars.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the fastening brackets on the rear pillars.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the shields on the hinged opening windows.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the sliding side door upper seals.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the switch mounting to the upper roof crossmember.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Working from the luggage compartment, withdraw the rear locating pin (1a) and use a blade of appropriate size to cut the adhesve sections (1b).

Work with great care in order not to damage the lining.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Working with care to avoid damage to the trim, undo the Velcro fastenings and arrange the trim on the seat head restraints.

Request the aid of a second operator in order to carry out this operation.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Release the wiring from the anchorage points to the ceiling lining and disconnect the electrical connections from the switches.

- Rotate the ceiling lining as necessary and withdraw through the tailgate.

Request the aid of a second operator in order to carry out this operation.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. When refitting, it is absolutely necessary to replace the front (1a) and rear (1b) ceiling stiffening hoops. These should have two anchorage pins (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Apply strips of sealing filler (1a) to the front stiffening hoop (1b).

2. Apply strips of sealing filler (2a) to the rear stiffening hoop (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Remove the remains of the Velcro fastenings from the lower sun roof frame;

- Degrease the parts in contact with the adhesive sections;

- Working with care, introduce the ceiling lining inside the passenger compartment;

Request the aid of a second operator in order to carry out this operation.

- Secure the wiring loom to the anchorage points.

- Connect the electrical connections.

- Position the trim on the ceiling over the locating pin and secure in place by pressing on the Velcro fastenings.

Request the aid of a second operator in order to carry out this operation.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the switch mounting to the upper roof crossmember.

- Position the mounting brackets on the centre pillars and secure them using the bolts.

- Position the mounting brackets on the rear pillars and secure them using the bolts.

- Fit the fastenings in position and secure them using the bolts.

- Place the shields on the hinged opening windows in position and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Position the sliding side door upper seals.

- Replace the seal around the front doors, side doors and tailgate.

- Fit both upper wind seals.
