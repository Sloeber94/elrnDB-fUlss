Removing

Refitting

- Position the automatic transmission assembly safely.

- Remove the torque converter retaining bracket.

1. Tighten the appropriate size grips (1a) and release the torque converter (1b) from the automatic transmission.

Even if the automatic transmission has been drained, the torque converter still contains a large amount of oil. A suitable container should be ready to collect it.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the torque converter in the housing in the automatic transmission and undo the grips.

- Fit the torque converter retaining bracket.

It is essential not to add any other fluid to the transmission other than the specified type. Before fitting a new torque converter, partly fill it with oil. Lubricate the gasket.

Before refitting the torque converter, ensure that the oil pump shaft and the turbine shaft can be turned by hand.

Position the torque converter in its seat on the automatic transmission, using the handles.

1. Check the correct position of the torque converter by fitting the locking device and checking that the position is the same as upon removal.

2. Then remove the torque converter.

The torque converter is fitted in two stages, by carrying out short alternate rotations:1, fit the spined stator seat to the intermediate shaft.2, fit the splined turbine impeller sheat on the input shaft.To fit the teeth into the seat on the oil pump, it may be necessary to raise the torque converter slightly to facilitate the operation.
