Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v, 3.0 24V V6; all production periods

Description

The engine radiator and air conditioner condenser cooling system consists of two fans operated at three different speeds achieved by means of an additional resistance:

  • the first speed is activated at an initial engine coolant temperature level or a certain air conditioning refrigerant fluid pressure;
  • the second speed is engaged at a higher temperature or pressure;
  • the third speed is engaged at even greater temperature or pressure values.

The fans are operated by means of three specific relays located near the radiator.
