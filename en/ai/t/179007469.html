Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 0010T84 AIR CONDITIONING system COOLANT - Dry and repressurize

1. Undo the fixing nut.

2. Disconnect the pipe (2a) from the connector on the condenser (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

- Raise the vehicle.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTIVE SHIELD UNDER ENGINE - R.R.

1. Undo the fixing nut.

2. Disconnect the pipe (2a) from the connector on the compressor (2b).

3. Open the retaining band and release the pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Remove the pipe from the compressor to the condenser.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the pipe between the compressor and the condenser and secure it using the special band.

Check the condition of the seals and replace them if necessary.

- Tighten the nut fixed to the connector on the compressor.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTIVE SHIELD UNDER ENGINE - R.R.

- Lower the vehicle.

- Tighten the nut fixed to the connector on the condenser.

See in Op. 0010T84 AIR CONDITIONING system COOLANT - Dry and repressurize

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
