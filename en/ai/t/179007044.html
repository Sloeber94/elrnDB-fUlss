Refitting

Removing

- Place the injectors in position and tighten the fixing bolts checking the torque.

- Connect the fuel return pipe to the injectors.

- Fit the new rigid fuel supply pipes from the single fuel manifold pipe to the individual injectors in place and tighten the connectors to the recommended torque.

- Place the fuel filter mounting in position and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the oil vapour pipes in place and renew the retaining bands.
