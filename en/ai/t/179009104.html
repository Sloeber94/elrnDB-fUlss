Operational Description

The automatic headlamp adjustment system is only supplied with the dipped headlamps on

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

The sensors K001 (pin 5) and K002 (pins 2, 5) that control the vehicle geometry, are supplied by the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 15 connector C) and the supply line is protected by fuse F09 which also protects the supply for the front light cluster F010 dipped headlamp bulb. Pins 1, 2 of sensor K001 and pin 1 of sensor K002 are earthed.

Sensors K001 and K002 provide the electronics incorporated in the headlamps F010 and F011, pin 7 with information concerning the vehicle geometry from pin 4. The electric motors automatically correct the light beam according to the information received.

The electronics are also supplied at pin 9 of F010 and F011 by the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 10 connector C) from the line protected by fuse F01 of the junction unit.

The self-diagnostic data for the two control units can be read by connecting to connector F10 - pin 13: it receives signals from pin 5 of F010 and F011 via the special diagnostic line.
