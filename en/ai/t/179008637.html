Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle may be equipped with two additional fog lights located in the front lights.

The fog lights are activated through the control on the steering column switch unit (steering wheel node control unit): with the lights on, the warning light in the instrument panel lights up.

The activation of the fog lights is managed by the Body Computer whilst the control takes place through the engine compartment junction unit.

The Body computer must receive the following input signals in order for the fog lights to remain activated:

- enablement signal with key in position M;

- command from steering column switch unit control;

- side lights on.

If the side lights are deactivated, the fog lights are also switched off: this operation is also carried out if the control has already been turned. Conversely, if the side lights are turned on again, the fog lights are also reactivated.

If the key is switched off (position S) and then switched back to position M, the ring nut on the steering column switch unit must be activated again to turn on the fog lights.
