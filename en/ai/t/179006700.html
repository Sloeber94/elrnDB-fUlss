INTERIOR LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Service fuse(s).
  • Wiring between Body Computer and courtesy light involved.
  • Supply for individual courtesy light.
  • Earth for individual courtesy light.

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

Move on to Step

1. Component defective

1. Replace the component involved (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

2. Connections interrupted

2. Restore the electrical connections.
