7040A31 FLAP ASSEMBLY - R R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7040H29 FRONT PILLAR LOWER COVER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- < R1 > [Right side]

Op. 7040A45 FACIA RH SIDE COVER - R R

Op. 7040G22 FACIA COMPARTMENT LIGHT SWITCH/BULB HOLDER - R+R

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

- Partly extract the compartment.

2. Remove the surround with telematic sockets.

3. Remove the compartment assembly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the surround with telematic sockets.

- Position the compartment assembly.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.

Op. 7040G22 FACIA COMPARTMENT LIGHT SWITCH/BULB HOLDER - R+R

Op. 7040A45 FACIA RH SIDE COVER - R R

Op. 7040H29 FRONT PILLAR LOWER COVER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- < R1 > [Right side]

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.
