Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The command for the electrical heating of the front seats is activated by switches I065 and I066 housed in the actual seats.

The body computer node M001 supplies the switches I065 and I065 and the seat heating control units M110 and M170 from pin 22 connector L (at pin 3 connector A).

The switches I065 and I066 provide the control units (pin 2 connector A) from pin 1 with a voltage that varies according to the position of the selector. Having received the signal from connector B, the control unit M170 and/or M110 activates the resistance for heating the cushion of the respective seat O046 and O047. The resistance for heating the backrest of the respective seat O048 and/or O049 is activated by connector C.

The power supplies for the control units M170 and M110 arrive from the lines protected by fuses F37 and F36 located in the additional fuse box B098.
