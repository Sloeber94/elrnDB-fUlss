Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: Gas discharge headlamps and MY 2005

The automatic headlamp adjustment system is only supplied with the dipped headlamps on.

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

The sensors K001 (pin 5) and K002 (pins 2 and 5) that control the vehicle geometry receive a power supply from the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 25 connector G).

Pin 1 of K002 and pins 1 and 2 of K001 are earthed.

Sensors K001 and K002 provide the electronics incorporated in the headlamps F010 and F011 (pin 7) with information concerning the vehicle geometry from pin 4. The electric motors automatically correct the light beam according to the information received.

The electronics are also supplied at pin 9 of F010 and F011 by the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 20 connector C) from the line protected by fuse F01 of the junction unit.

The self-diagnostic data for the two control units can be read by connecting to the multiple diagnostic coupling R010 (pin 13): it receives the signals from pin 5 of F010 and F011.
