Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

When the tailgate is opened the courtesy lights (one on each side) located in the luggage compartment come on automatically.

The courtesy lights go out gradually when the tailgate is closed.

The courtesy lights go out after a few minutes to protect the battery charge.

The light inside the glove compartment comes on - with the key in position M - when it is opened and goes out when it is closed.

The lights located under each of the two sun visors come on when the visor is opened; both the courtesy lights at the side of the vanity mirror come on.

The light for illuminating the ashtray is on with the key in position M.
