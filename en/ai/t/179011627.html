Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is prepared for the installation of a "CONNECT NAV" system: it involves a radio built into the CONNECT device and a navigator with maps.

The telephone system includes:

- GSM module and SIM CARD reader built into the radio;

- navigator module with maps incorporated in the radio;

- hands free function via the radio speakers;

- microphone located in the front courtesy light;

- GSM/GPS exterior aerial.

The navigation system makes it possible to know the position of the vehicle in the road network, at any time. In this way the driver is guided to the required destination step by step by means of voice messages and visual information. The system also provides additional information for reaching general services, namely: hotels, carparks, restaurants, chemists, service stations, hospitals, authorized workshops, railway stations, airports, etc.

The navigation system uses GPS (Global Positioning System) satellite technology consisting of an aerial that allows the GPS satellite location of the vehicle: The aerial is connected to a receiver located in the integrated unit by means of a special shielded cable. The aerial is fitted at the rear of the roof and has two functions (GSM/GPS): it incorporates a GSM aerial when the CONNECT system is also required.

Vehicle position is identified by analysing GPS data together with speedometer and reverse gear signals received from the vehicle.

The navigation CD-ROM drive is located in the radio assembly.

the navigation system only works with the CD-ROM inserted in the special drive; it is therefore possible to listen to a music CD during navigation. In this case, the navigation function requires the map CD to be inserted whilst carrying out the route calculation procedures.

The main radio controls are duplicated on the steering wheel allowing the driver to operate the radio without taking their hands off the steering wheel.

The special controls for the Connect function are on a special control knob located in the centre of the dashboard next to the climate control system controls.

A cooling fan ensures the necessary exchange of air in the area of the radio, preventing it from overheating.

The Connect radio system is, to all intents and purposes, a CAN node and is connected to the body computer, the instrument panel and the multifunction screen where information relating to the radio is displayed.

A special CD cartridge allows up to 6 CDs to be loaded which are then selected through the radio controls. The CD changer is located in the dashboard console.

The following is also available, depending on the trim level:

- radio with CD player;

See E3510 RADIO

- "CONNECT Nav" device (with navigator with pictograms) described in this section;

- "CONNECT Nav +" device (with navigator with maps).

See E4090 NAVIGATOR

All the details of the specifications and operations of these radio systems are given in the "OWNER'S HANDBOOK".
