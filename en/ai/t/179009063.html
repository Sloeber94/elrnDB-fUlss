Operational Description

The control unit for the seats/mirrors with a memory M076 manages the function of adjusting the seats according to the position memorized and also controls the movement of the door mirrors using information transmitted the body computer M001 via the CAN

See E3032 DOOR MIRROR ADJUSTMENT

The seats/mirrors with memory control unit M076 receives a supply at pins B7 and B8 connector A along the line protected by fuse F32 located in the additional fuse box B098; it is earthed at pins C8 and C7 connector A and converses, via the CAN, with the body computer control unit M001 from pins C5 and A5 connector A.

The supply via the CAN arrives at pin A7 from the line protected by fuse F1 of the body computer M001.

The adjustment of the driver's seat is controlled by the switch H066 which is connected rom pins 1, 2, 3 and 4 to pins A3, B2, B3 and C2 connector B of the control unit M076.

The adjustment signals are managed by the seats/mirrors with memory control unit M076

The left front seat sliding adjustment motor N127, the left front seat front height adjustment motor N126 and the left front seat backrest motor N132 receive the control signals from pins B7 and A7, A1 and C8, B1 and C2 connector B of control unit M076.

The height position sensor K178 and the sliding sensor K179 and the backrest position sensor K180 refer the seat position variations to the feedback signals: the three sensors receive a supply from pin C6 of connector B of M076, a reference earth at pin C4 and send a signal to pins C5, A5 and B4 of connector B of M076, respectively.
