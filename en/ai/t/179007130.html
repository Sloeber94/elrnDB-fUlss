Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

The particle can be reconditioned, use the original packaging to send the replaced filter following the instructions given.

1. Undo the connector (1a) and the fixing bolt (1b) and move the pipe (1c) aside.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the particle filter to the catalytic converter.

3. Undo the nut (3a) and loosen the metal collar (3b).

4. Release the section of the silencer pipe from the rubber fastening.

5. Remove the particle filter complete with gasket.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the filter (1a) in position and screw in the fastenings (1b) without tightening them.

Before refitting, clean the metal surfaces in the contact with the gasket thoroughly and check that the catalyzers flanges and the filter are correctly aligned.

2. Secure the silencer pipe to the rubber fastening.

3. Renew the metal collar and tighten the fixing nut to the recommended torque.

Value - daNm
