Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

See in Op. 1044B10

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the fuel filter mounting (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connectors for the fuel pipes.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the fuel temperature sensor.

2. Undo the retaining bolts.

3. Remove the fuel temperature sensor

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the sensor and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection for the sensor.

- Connect the rapid connectors for the fuel pipes.

- Place the fuel filter mounting in position and tighten the fixing bolts.

See in Op. 1044B10

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
