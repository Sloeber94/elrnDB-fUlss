Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Description

An electronic control system supervises and governs all engine parameters to optimise performance and fuel consumption by means of a real-time response to different service conditions.

Diesel engines are equipped with a COMMON RAIL type injection system.

This features the use of an electronically controlled high injection pressure.

The supply of fuel, known as the pilot injection, is optimized by the electronic control unit by means of the injector rather than managed by the pump.

Depending on the signals received from numerous sensors, the control unit controls the injectors connected to it managing the following systems:

  • fuel supply;
  • air supply;
  • heater plugs;
  • accelerator pedal;
  • engine cooling;
  • fuel pre-heating;
  • operation of the particle filter (FAP).(Wiring Diagram ) E5070 FAP PARTICLE FILTER SYSTEM

The rail is connected to the various injectors by means of pipes. The injector opening is implemented by a solenoid valve controlled by the engine management node.

Depending on the engine operating conditions, the NCM manages:

  • the pressure in the rail,
  • the high pressure pump flow rate,
  • the moment and opening time for each injector.

The common rail system manages different fuel injectors for each injector cycle for each engine cycle:

  • the moment and opening time for each injector.
  • pilot injection,
  • main injection,
  • post-injection (when needed).
