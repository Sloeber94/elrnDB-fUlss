For ground cylinder heads, it is necessary to correct any excess valve height. This operation requires the fitting of special undersize valves available as spares.

The nominal cylinder head height is 133 ± 0.05 mm; the maximum repair height is 132.6 ± 0.05 mm.

The flatness tolerance must be within 0.05 mm.

1. Measure the piston projection (1a) from the cylinder head gasket support plane using a dial guage and the tool (1b) following the instructions given below:

- place the piston in question at T.D.C.;

- bring the pointer in contact with the control point, shown in the diagram and zero the dial gauge;

- move the pointer on the engine block plane and take a reading for S1;

- bring the pointer in contact with the control point on the opposite side, shown in the diagram and zero the dial gauge;

- move the pointer on the engine block plane and take a reading for S2;

The piston projection in question is equal to the average value for the two readings

S = (S1 + S2) / 2

Repeat the above procedure for the remaining three pistons and check that the maximum difference between the projections is less than 0.07 mm.
