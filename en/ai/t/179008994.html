Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The body computer node controls the operation of the driver's and passenger side rear electric window motors.

The electric windows for the sliding rear doors are activated either by means of the switches located on the driver's front door or through those on each of the rear doors.

The electric rear windows are manually operated (the glass only moves up or down when the button is pressed).

For safety reasons, a special "locking" button, located on the driver's front door, allows the power supply to the switches on the rear doors to be cut off (in addition to opening/closing the sliding doors and the opening rear roofs to prevent operation, for example if children are present.

See E3060 SUN ROOF

See E3095 SLIDING SIDE DOORS

The rear electric windows are also equipped with an anti-crush safety system. The control unit can detect the presence of obstacles while the window is closing. In this case, the system stops the window in its travel and reverses the direction of motion immediately.

With the key in position S, the windows remain activated for around 1 minute.

When the remote control key is pressed to activate the passenger compartment ventilation function, the front and rear side windows descend fully to allow outside air to enter the passenger compartment.

See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING
