1. Disconnect the vacuum pipe (1a) for the E.G.R. valve and the coolant pipes (1b) from the E.G.R. valve heat exchanger by adjusting the fastening bands.

2. Undo the fixing nuts (2a) and remove the E.G.R. valve assembly and heat exchanger (2b).

- Separate the E.G.R. valve from the heat exchanger at the bench.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

- Connect the vacuum pipes to the EGR valve.

- Connect the coolant pipes to the E.G.R. valve heat exchanger and renew the fastening bands.
