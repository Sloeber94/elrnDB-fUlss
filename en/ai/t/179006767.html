Applies to: all engines; all production periods

There is wind noise at the front whilst the vehicle is moving.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

WIND NOISE LOCATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out a detailed wind noise location check identifying the area where the wind noise is coming from;in particular, check:

  • that the front wing attachment triangular cover is correctly fastened and that the seal is not distorted
  • that the wheel arch liner under the wing is correctly positioned and not distorted
  • the condition of the door housing seal and that it sticks correctly to the actual door.

Move on to Step

1. Front wing attachment triangular cover not fastened and seal distorted

1. Fasten/replace the sealing plug under the cover

2. Liner under wing not correctly positioned or distorted

2. Reposition the liner and, if necessary, replace it

3. Wind noise coming from the rear of the front door three-lobe joint (upper door hinge)

3. Check that the wind noise buffer is fitted in the area of the three-lobe joint and reposition it, if necessary

4. Door housing seal damaged and not sticking to the door

4. Renew the seal and adjust the door hinges so that the door is correctly aligned

5. Wind noise coming from the front of the bonnet

Move on to Step

2

BONNET CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • that the sound insulation under the bonnet is correctly fitted,
  • that the bonnet is correctly aligned,
  • the condition of the weatherstrip incorporated in the trim under the windscreen, making sure that it is correctly fitted

Move on to Step

1. sound insulation under the bonnet not correctly fitted

1. refit correctly

2. bonnet not aligned

2. align the bonnet

3. seal damaged and/or not correctly fitted

3. refit correctly

3

FIREPROOF BULKHEAD CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • that the plugs are present and correctly fitted
  • that the cable duct rubber covers are correctly fitted
  • that the sealing is correct

Move on to Step

Fireproof bulklhead faulty

Renew fireproof bulkhead

4

WINDSCREEN WIND NOISE LOCATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out a test to locate the area of the windscreen where the wind noise is coming from;also check that the windscreen surround seal is intact and sticking properly to the housing;

Move on to Step

1. The wind noise is located in the top part of the windscreen and the area where the windscreen is detached is too extensive

1. Carry out the complete removing and refitting cycle for the windscreen

2. The wind noise is located at the bottom of the windscreen and the windscreen wiper blades are not correctly positioned

2. Position the windscreen wiper blades using the reference points marked on the windscreen

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
