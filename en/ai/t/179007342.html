Op. 4450B13 REAR WHEELS(2) R R

- Mark adjustment nut position.

1. Unscew adjustment nut (1a) and prise up the wire leading from the handbrake (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Prise up the control cable from the left rear brake caliper.

2. Release the brake control cable from the fastenings and remove.

- Carry out the same procedure for the right rear caliper brake control cable.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the brake control cables.

- Fit the rear brake caliper brake control cables.

- Restore the brake control cable fastenings.

- Fit the wire leading from the handbrake and tighten the adjustment nut.
