Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Loosen the bolt fixing the engine coolant reservoir and move it forwards towards the mechanic.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection for the coolant level sensor.

3. Undo it by around 90° in an anti-clockwise direction and remove the coolant level sensor.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Tighten the coolant level sensor in the housing on the coolant reservoir.

- Connect the electrical connection for the coolant level sensor.

- Tighten the bolt fixing the coolant reservoir.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Loosen the bolt fixing the engine coolant reservoir and move it forwards towards the mechanic.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection for the coolant level sensor.

3. Undo the coolant level sensor by around 90° in an anti-clockwise direction and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Tighten the coolant level sensor in the housing on the coolant reservoir.

- Connect the electrical connection for the coolant level sensor.

- Tighten the bolt fixing the coolant reservoir.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
