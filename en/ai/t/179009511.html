Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The tyre pressure monitoring system is managed by control unit M047.

The aerials involved in receiving the signals for the right front wheel (pins 1 and 2), the left front wheel (pins 1 and 2) and the rear wheels (pin 1) are connected to the control unit M047 (pins 12-15, 13-14, 10-11 respectively).

Control unit M047 (pin 7) receives a power supply from the Body computer node M001 (pin 2 connector F) through a line protected by fuse F09 and communicates (pins 9 and 18), via the CAN line, with M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D), with the instrument panel E050 (pins 6 and 12) and with the milometer warning lights control unit M140 (pins 2 and 5).

The control units M050 (pin 4) and M140 (pin 13) receive a power supply from the Body computer node M001 (pin 6 connector D) via a line protected by fuse F14.
