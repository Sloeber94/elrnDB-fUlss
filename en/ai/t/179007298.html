Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Acting on the adjustment nut, loosen the handbrake control cable.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the handbrake cable from the mounting on the rear caliper.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the rear brake caliper to the bracket.

2. Move the rear brake caliper (2a) away from the brackets and remove the left and right brake pads (2b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Using the special tool, retract the rear brake caliper cylinders.

Positioning brake caliper cylinders

Fit new brake pads with the same specifications as those removed from the housing in the bracket.

- Fit the brake pads inside the brake caliper.

- Fit the rear brake caliper on the disc.

Handle the caliper carefully so that the upper wheel bolt is not subjected to force

- Tighten the bolts fixing the rear brake caliper to the bracket to torque.

- Connect the handbrake cable to the rear brake caliper mounting.

Op. 3380A04 HANDBRAKE - CHECK TRAVEL

-

Brake with the engine running, gently and gradually, before driving the vehicle on the road.
