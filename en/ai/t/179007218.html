Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the pressure plate and the clutch disc (1b).

If it is being reused, mark the position of the clutch control mechanism in relation to the engine flywheel.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Check that there are no oil leaks at the crankshaft (flywheel side); if there are, proceed with dismantling the flywheel and replacing the gasket.

1. If there are signs of wear, overheating or deep grooves on the pressure plate, replace the assembly.

2. If the linings are excessively worn, the complete driven disc must be replaced.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

Fit the driven disc and the pressure plate and align them using the clutch centering tool.
