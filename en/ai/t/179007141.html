Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on the lift.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTIVE SHIELD UNDER ENGINE - R.R.

- Position a suitable container for collecting the coolant.

1. Open the fastening bands (1a) and disconnect the air inlet and outlet hoses (1b).

2. Position the terminals on the coolant inlet and outlet pipes.

3. Disconnect the coolantinlet and outlet pipes.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the bolt fixing the heat exchanger.

2. Release and remove the water/air heat exchanger.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the water/air heat exchanger and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Fit the heat exchanger air inlet and outlet hoses and tighten the fastening bands.

- Fit and secure the coolant inlet and outlet hoses to the water/air heat exchanger.

- Remove the terminals positioned on coolant pipes.

See in Op. 7055B54 PROTECTIVE SHIELD UNDER ENGINE - R.R.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

- Check the coolant level and top up.
