1. Undo the retaining bolts.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Adjust the clips and disconnect the drainage pipe.

2. Undo the retaining bolt.

3. Release and remove the windscreen base trim on the left side.

- Do the same to remove the windscreen base trim on the right side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the windscreen base side trims.

- Connect the drainage pipes.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.

- Position the centre windscreen base trim.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.
