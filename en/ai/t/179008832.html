Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is equipped with a horn system consisting of two horns with different tones: one high tone horn and one low tone horn; the two horns sound simultaneously.

The horns are operated by pressing the sides of the steering wheel cover (around the Air Bag).

For safety reasons, the horns can be operated at any time even with the key in position S (or switched off).
