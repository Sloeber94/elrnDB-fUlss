Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The adjustment of the light beam for the headlamps depending on the load is produced using the adjustment device located directly on the headlamps. This device tilts the light beam in order to lower it if the vehicle is particularly laden or to raise it if the load lessens.

A rotary selector on the left side of the dashboard allows the headlights to be adjusted in four positions:

- position 0 - one or two persons in the front seats, full fuel tank, fully equipped (vehicle in running order);

- position 1 - five persons;

- position 2 - five persons with luggage compartment laden;

- position 3 - seven or eight persons with luggage compartment laden, or driver and maximum load, all in the luggage compartment.

Adjust the alignment of the light beams each time the weight of the load transported changes.

The device is enabled only with the dipped beams on.

The body computer supplies the adjustment motors incorporated in the headlamps.

On the version with gas discharge headlamps the light beam is adjusted automatically: there is no rotary selector on the left side of the dashboard.
