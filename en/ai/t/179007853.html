Refitting

- Place the front seat in the front end of travel position.

- Place the seat backrest in the front end of travel position.

- Release the backrest rear cover circlip.

- Release the backrest rear cover from the joint covers.

1. Release the lower fastening clips for the backrest rear cover.

2. Release the upper fastening clips for the backrest rear cover.

3. Remove the backrest rear cover for the front seat, complete with rear pocket panel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the rear pocket panel from the lower fastening clip.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the rear pocket panel from the side fastening clips.

2. Using the special tool, remove the lower rear pocket panel fastening clips.

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the pocket for the pocket panel.

- Fit the rear pocket panel support frame.

- Fit the rear pocket panel pretensioner.

- Attach the lower clips fastening the rear pocket panel to the backrest rear cover.

- Attach the side clips fastening the rear pocket panel to the backrest rear cover.
