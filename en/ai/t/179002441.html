Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

All electrical systems and circuits are supplied by battery A001.

The different power supplies to the various devices is managed by engine bay junction units B001 and body computer node M001.

Several MAXIFUSES are located in the engine compartment junction unit B001:

- MF01 (pin 1 connector D) supplies the climate control system control unit;

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

- MF02 (pin 2 connector D) supplies the ABS/ESP control unit;

See E7020 ABS

See E7023 ESP

- MF03 (pin 3 connector D) supplies the ESP control unit;

See E7023 ESP

- MF04 and MF05 (pins 4 and 5 connector D) supply the Body computer node M001 (pins 1 and 2 connector A respectively).

Some of the optional system power supply lines are protected by MAXIFUSES grouped together in junction unit B005 located next to the battery and directly connected to it:

- MF01 (pin 1 connector B) supplies the central locking system;

See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING

- MF02 (pin 2 connector B) supplies the sliding side door system.

See E3095 SLIDING SIDE DOORS

There is an additional maxifuse box B006:

- MF1 and MF4 (pin 1 connector B and pins 2 connector C) supply the engine cooling fans;

See E5020 ENGINE COOLING

- MF3 (pin 2 connector B) supplies the starting system;

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

- MF6 (pin 2 connector D) supplies the engine electronic management system;

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Other fuses are grouped together in an additional fuse box B098:

- F40 and F39 (pins G40 and G39 connector B) supply the current socket circuits;

See E3070 CIGAR LIGHTER / CURRENT SOCKET

- F38 (pin G38 connector B) supplies the main beam lights system;

See E2012 MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS

- F37 and F36 (pins G37 and G36 connector B) supply the seat heating circuits;

See E3075 HEATED SEATS

- F32 and F33 (pins G32 and G33 connector B) supply the seat adjustment circuits;

See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS

- F34 and F35 (pins G34 and G35 connector B) supply the sun roof adjustment circuits;

See E3060 SUN ROOF

- F31 (pin G31) provides the power signal to the brake pedal I030.

Lastly, the ignition switch H001 is connected (pins 3, 1, 2) to the Body computer node M001 Pins 6 and 4 connector G and pin 5 connector D respectively).
