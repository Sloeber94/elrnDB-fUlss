Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Sliding side door found to be stiff to close and noisy when vehicle is in motion.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Wtih the door handle in open position, carry out various side door close/open manoeuvres and check for interference with the striker

Move on to Step

The striker is incorrectly adjusted

Adjust the striker

2

CHECK WITH DOOR OPEN

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Close/open the catch by hand. Check operation and ensure lubrication is present

Move on to Step

1. The catch is not secured and difficult to open/close

1. Secure the catch

2. No lubricant present

2. Lubricate with the specified grease

3

CHECK DOOR ALIGNMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check door close without the striker by positioning the door against the vehicle body and checking the door is square against the door compartment

Move on to Step

The sliding door is slightly out of squareness

Adjust and/or resquare the sliding door

4

CHECK WITH DOOR CLOSED

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the alignment of the sliding door with the front door and rear wing is linear without protrusions

Move on to Step

The upper front of the sliding door protrudes and the weather strip rubs excessively against the front door

Adjust the upper locating device, lubricate the weather strip with the specified grease. Add washers to the upper carriage if necessary

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
