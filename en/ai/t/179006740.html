Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The window does not move at all when the switch in the rear door is operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The Body Computer Node receives the window opening/closing command directly from the individual switches located on the rear doors. The Body Computer Node receives the opening/closing command, via the VAN CAR 2 line, from the switch panel located on the driver's front door. Two special fuses, housed in the junction unit under the dashboard, protect the electric rear windows supply system.The networks go on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
