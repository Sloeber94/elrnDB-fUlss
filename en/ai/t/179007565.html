Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Lift up the carpet and undo the fixing bolt.

2. Disconnect the battery earth lead.

3. Release and remove the battery earth lead.

Removing

- Position the battery earth lead.

- Lift up the carpet and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
