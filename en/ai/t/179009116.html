Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Headlamp alignment adjustment is managed by a dedicated selector H015. Pin 2 of H015 (like pin 9 of the front light clusters F010 and F011) receive a power supply from pin 2 of connector B of the Body computer node M001. This power supply is only provided with the dipped headlamps on.

Pin 3 of H015 is earthed together with pin 8 of connector B of the Body computer node M001, whilst a variable voltage signal is sent from pin 1 to pin 7 of the left front light cluster F010 and the right front light cluster F011.

The headlamp alignment correction motors inside the front light clusters contain a transducer that estabilishes the travel to be completed on the basis of adjustment signal voltage. The dipped headlamps are connected to pin 1 of the front light clusters F010 and F011, supplied from pins 25 and 26 respectively of connector G of the engine compartment junction unit B001.

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS
