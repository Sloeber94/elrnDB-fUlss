Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The following diagram shows the various earths on the car. The associated circuits are shown for each earth.

This makes it easy to identify the earthed circuits on an individual line: the task of searching for faults and problems is facilitated in cases where several functions are involved, for example the oxidation of an earth can exclude different circuits and numerous functions.

Several earths belonging to the same wiring are connected to one another through welding. This allows all the electrical components on the same wiring to be correctly earthed even if a particular earth point cannot allow effective contact with the vehicle body.
