Removing

Refitting

- Proceed as follows to remove the left front brake disc shield.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R

Op. 3310A48 FRONT BRAKE DISC - R R

- < R1 > [Left side]

1. Undo the remaining retaining bolt.

2. Remove the brake disc guard.

- Proceed in the same way to remove the right front brake disc shield.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Proceed as follows to refit the left front brake disc shield.

- Fit the brake disc shield and tighten the retainining bolt.

Op. 3310A48 FRONT BRAKE DISC - R R

- < R1 > [Left side]

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL R R

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Proceed in the same way to refit the right front brake disc shield.
