Refitting

Op. 7040A44 SEAT INTERIOR GUARD - R R

- < R1 > [Left side]

Op. 7040A47 ODDMENTS POCKET UNDER DASHBOARD - R.R.

Op. 5010B68 AIR DUCT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT BETWEEN TIMING CASING CONNECTOR AND FAN FOR REAR SEATS - R.R.

- < R1 > [Left side]

Op. 7040A65 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT BOX LOCK , ONE , R R

- < R1 > [Left side]

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and (1b).

2. Remove the dashboard crossmember reinforcement bracket.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
