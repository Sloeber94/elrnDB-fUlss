Removing

Refitting

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7040A26 STEERING COLUMN TRIM - R+R

1. Disconnect the electrical connection.

- Release and extract the electronic key aerial.

- Release the wiring from the steering wheel adjustment plates.

2. Remove the electronic key aerial.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the wiring between the steering wheel adjustment plates.

- Place the electronic key aerial in position and secure it.

- Connect the electrical connection.

Op. 7040A26 STEERING COLUMN TRIM - R+R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.
