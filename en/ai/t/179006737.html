Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The front windows, driver's side and passenger side, do not work when the switches are pressed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The supply for the door Nodes which operate the electric window motors is protected by specific fuses housed in the Body Computer Node. The networks go on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
