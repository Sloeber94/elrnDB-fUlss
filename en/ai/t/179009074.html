Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle can be fitted with electrically adjustable front seats, in particular:

  • a button adjusts the lengthwise movement (forwards/backwards) and height adjustment;
  • a button adjusts the backrest.

Each of the adjustments is carried out by a specific electric motor.

The backrest lumbar setting can be mechanically adjusted on the driver's seat.

The adjustments can be made with the ignition key in position M and for about 1 minute after the key is turned to position S.

The circuit for each seat is protected by a specific fuse, located in the additional fuse box.
