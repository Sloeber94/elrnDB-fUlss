Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: bumper removed, rear crossmember removed, rear lining removed.

The procedure in question involves the complementary operation of replacing the assembled tailgate foot, the assembled rear wheel arch, the rear panel and the rear panel box section.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
