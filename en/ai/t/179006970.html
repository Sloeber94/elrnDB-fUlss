Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

1. Undo and remove the fuel filler pipe cap.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the fuel flap (1b) from the housing.

2. Acting on the throttle (2a), separate the flap from the opening cable (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and remove the filler pipe housing seal (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the fuel filler pipe (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the breather pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the submerged pump.

2. Disconnect the fuel supply and return pipes from the rapid connectors.

3. Disconnect the connector.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Position a hydraulic jack with a suitable support under the fuel tank.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the tank to the bodyshell.

2. Lower the hydraulic jack and remove the fuel tank.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the fuel tank on the support tool and fit it in the special housing.

Tighten the bolts fixing fuel tank support belts to torque.

Remove the hydraulic jack and support from underneath the tank.

Connect the electrical connection for the submerged pump.

Connect the fuel supply and return pipes to the rapid connectors.

Connect the connector.

Connect the breather pipe.

Position the filler pipe in the housing and tighten the fixing bolts.

Fit the seal in the filler housing and tighten the fixing bolt.

Connect the flap opening control cable to the flap and titghten the bolts fixing the fuel flap to the bodyshell.

Fit the fuel filler pipe plug.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

Connect the negative battery terminal.

Remove the vehicle from the lift. Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

Op. 1040A20 FUEL TANK - R + R AND DECANT FUEL FOR VERSIONS WITH EVAPORATION CONTROL SYSTEM

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Position the reservoir drain device in the filler fitting.

To facilitate the operation, raise the vehicle slightly at the left rear.Proceed to drain.

The fuel contains an additive required by the particulate filter.it is therefore important to put back all the fuel removed from the tank.Because this fuel contains an additive, it CANNOT be used for engines without a particulate filter..

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

1. Undo and remove the fuel filler pipe cap.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the fuel flap (1b) from the housing.

2. Acting on the throttle (2a), separate the flap from the opening cable (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and remove the filler pipe housing seal (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the fuel filler pipe (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the breather pipe.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the submerged pump.

2. Disconnect the fuel supply and return pipes from the rapid connectors.

3. Disconnect the pipe connector.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connectors from the FAP additive reservoir.

Disconnect the electrical connection for the particulate filter system injector.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Position a hydraulic jack with a suitable support under the fuel tank.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the tank to the bodyshell.

2. Lower the hydraulic jack and remove the fuel tank.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the fuel tank on the support tool and fit it in the special housing.

Tighten the bolts fixing fuel tank support belts to torque.

Remove the hydraulic jack and support from underneath the tank.

Connect the electrical connection for the submerged pump.

Connect the fuel supply and return pipes to the rapid connectors.

Connect the rapid pipe connectors to the particulate filter system additive reservoir.

Disconnect the electrical connection for the particulate filter system injector.

Connect the connector.

Connect the breather pipe.

Position the filler pipe in the housing and tighten the fixing bolts.

Fit the seal in the filler housing and tighten the fixing bolt.

Connect the flap opening control cable to the flap and titghten the bolts fixing the fuel flap to the bodyshell.

If the fuel drawn off during draining cannot be reused (presence of water or impurities)Proceed as follows:- Position the filler fitting cap.- Turn on the contact for 5 seconds.- Turn off the contact- Add the fuel.This operation allows the additive calculator to inject the quantity of additive corresponding to the amount of diesel added.

Fit the fuel filler pipe cap.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

Connect the negative battery terminal.

Remove the vehicle from the lift.
