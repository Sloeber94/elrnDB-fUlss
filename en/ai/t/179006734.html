The climate control recirculation does not work in any conditions (manual/automatic)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

Connect the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) to the diagnostic socket. Check:That there are no errors in the climate control node (NCL)

Move on to Step

1. Error in the control unit.

1. Check the NCL supply connections. Move on to Step

2. Recirculation motor error.

Move on to Step

3. No communication with the NBC.

3. Restore the connection between the climate control node (NCL) and the NBC

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

Recirculation system stuck.

Move on to Step

MOTOR ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ALL OK
