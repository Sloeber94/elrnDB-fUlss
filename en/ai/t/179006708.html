J21 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the button in the control panel is pressed, the heated rear windscreen defrosting does not work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The supply for the heated rear windscreen is protected by a specific fuse and operated by a relay, both housed in the body computer node.The networks go on stand-by 30 mins after the engine has been switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
