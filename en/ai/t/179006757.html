Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Tailgate found to be difficult to close

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out various tailgate closure/opening manoeuvres and ensure tailgate movements are uniform and centred

Move on to Step

Closure is difficult (the tailgate must be slammed closed)

Adjust the striker

2

CHECK WITH TAILGATE CLOSED

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the tailgate closed, carry out a visual check of the openings. Check they are uniform and aligned with the roof and also the tail-lights

Move on to Step

When closed, the tailgate is shifted to the left

Loosen the striker, check the cause of the shift and adjust the striker

3

CHECK WITH TAILGATE OPEN

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out various catch closure/opening manoeuvres by hand

An occasional malfunction is detected in the catch

Replace the catch
