B13 ENGINE STARTS WITH DIFFICULTY'

Path

Kind

all engines; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

The engine, particularly when cold, starts with difficulty and only after repeated attempts. When the key is turned the instruments light up as normal, the starter motor turns, but the engine is difficult to start.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • that there are no obstructions in the air filter
  • condition of the air intake circuit;
  • diesel pipe seal
  • absence of fuel, water, paraffin or dirt in the diesel filter.
  • correct operation of the exhaust manifolds

Move on to Step

1. Air filter faulty

1. Replace the filter

2. Air intake circuit faulty

2. Restore correct operation

3. Leaks from the diesel pipes

3. Adjust pipe tightness to correct setting and replace if necessary

4. Water, petrol, paraffin or dirt found to be present

4. Clean the tank and pipes and replace the diesel filter cartridge

5. Obstruction in the exhaust system

5. Replace the damaged system component
