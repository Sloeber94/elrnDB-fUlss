Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The two reversing lights come on with the ignition ON even when reverse gear is not enaged.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The reversing lights are controlled by the Body Computer Node BSI.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for the presence of one or more of the following errors using the Examiner in the Body Computer ambient:

  • Reversing switch error
  • Inconsistent operation

Move on to Step

1. Errors detected at the reverse switch.

Move on to Step

2. Operation inconsistency error.

2. Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment.

2

REVERSE SWITCH ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Reversing lights protective fuse(s).
  • Wiring between junction unit and reversing lights switch.
  • Reversing lights control switch.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Move on to Step

1. Protective fuse/s blown

1. Replace the component involved (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

2. Wiring defective

2. 1. Restore wiring

3. Reversing lights control switch faulty

3. Restore the correct operation of the reversing lights control switch

3

REVERSING LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Wiring between junction unit and bulbs.
  • Condition of the bulb holder assembly in question.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Move on to Step

1. Wiring defective.

1. Repair wiring.

2. Bulb holders damaged.

2. Repair or replace the rear light cluster.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
