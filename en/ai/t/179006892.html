Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Position a suitable container for collecting the engine oil.

1. Unscrew the engine oil drain plug and remove.

The engine oil used contains substances that are harmful to the environment. These substances should be placed in specially designed containers.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the crankcase sump (2b).

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods
