2. Undo the bolt fixing the gearbox to the engine.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and (1b) fixing the automatic transmission to the engine.

Support the automatic transmission assembly appropriately to prevent the gearbox from falling.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods

Fit a suitable size bracket (1a) in the housing for the rpm sensor to stop the torque converter (1b) sliding.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods

1. Detach and remove the automatic transmission from the engine with the help of a second mechanic and place it safely.

A retaining bracket must be fitted to prevent the torque converter from coming out whilst moving the gearbox.

Do not use connectors for moving the gearbox, but attach the automatic transmission unit, using a suitable lift, to the retaining plate located at the side of the heat exchanger.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods
