Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The adjustment of the headlamp light beam according to the load is carried out by means of a special device (located inside the front light cluster) which tilts it appropriately, lowering the light beam if the vehicle is heavily laden and raising it if the load lessens.

A rotary selector on the left side of the dashboard allows the headlights to be adjusted from positions 0 to 3:

  • position 0 - one or two persons in the front seats, full fuel tank, fully equipped (vehicle in running order);
  • position 1 - five persons;
  • position 2 - five persons with luggage compartment full;
  • position 3 - seven or eight persons with luggage compartment full, or driver and maximum load, all in the luggage compartment.

The device is enabled only with the dipped beams on.

A special relay, located in the body computer, supplies the adjustment motors incorporated inside the headlights.

on versions with gas discharge headlamps, the light beam is adjusted automatically and there is no rotary selector on the left side
