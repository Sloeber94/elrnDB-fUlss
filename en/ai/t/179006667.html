STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • condition of the air intake circuit;
  • that there are no faults in the air filter
  • the seal of the diesel pipes
  • the accelerator pedal travel
  • that there are no obstructions in the exhaust pipe;
  • no water, petrol, paraffin or dirt in the fuel tank and fuel filter

Move on to Step

1. Fault in the air intake circuit

1. Renew the air intake circuit

2. Air filter blocked

2. Replace the filter element

3. Leaks from diesel pipes

3. Eliminate the leaks

4. Accelerator pedal does not complete its full travel

4. Eliminate the cause of the problem

5. obstructions in the exhaust system

5. Replace the damaged system component

6. Water, petrol, paraffin or dirt found to be present

6. Clean the tank and pipes and replace the filter element
