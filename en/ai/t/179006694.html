Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When reverse gear is engaged, with the ignition ON, one of the reversing lights does not come on. N.B. The reversing lights are controlled by the Body Computer Node BSI.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

LIGHT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the reversing light bulb and associated bulb holder

Move on to Step

Bulb damaged

1. Replace the bulb

2. Repair or replace the right rear light cluster

3

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for the presence of one or more of the following errors using the Examiner in the Body Computer ambient:

  • Reversing switch error
  • Inconsistent operation

Move on to Step

1. Errors detected at the reverse switch.

1. Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment

2. Operation inconsistency error.

2. Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment.

3

REVERSING LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Reversing lights protective fuse(s).
  • Wiring between junction unit and bulbs.
  • Condition of the bulb holder assembly in question.
  • Earth for bulbs and/or bulb holder.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

Move on to Step

1. Protective fuse/s blown.

1. Replace the component involved (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

2. Wiring defective.

2. Repair wiring.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

3. Bulb holders damaged.

3. Repair or replace the rear light cluster.

4. Bulb holder earth faulty.

Renew bulb holder earth.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
