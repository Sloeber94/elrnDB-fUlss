Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before removing and refitting the Air Bag control unit the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it; (2) wait for 2 minutes before continuing; (3) remove the battery compartment cover, disconnect the negative battery terminal and insulate the leads; (4) wait for 5 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS (see assembly 5580C - AIR BAG SYSTEM).
