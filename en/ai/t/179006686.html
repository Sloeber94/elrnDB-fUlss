Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Only the third brake lights do not work when the brake pedal is pressed with the key ON.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The third brake lights are connected to the right rear brake light branch

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the third brake light does not come on whilst the other side lights do by pressing/releasing the brake pedal

Move on to Step

Left and/or right bulb holder assembly faulty.

See table I15 (SIDE) BRAKE LIGHTS NOT WORKING

1

CENTRE BRAKE LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the five individual bulbs and the bulb holder assembly.

Move on to Step

1. Bulbs damaged

1. Replace the individual bulbs

2. Bulb holder assembly faulty

2. Repair and/or replace the third brake light

2

CENTRE LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity of the wiring between the junction unit and the bulb holder

See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Wiring damaged

Renew the electrical connections

3

CENTRE LIGHT ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the earth connection for the bulb holder is correct.

See E2021 BRAKE LIGHTS

Move on to Step

Earth connection damaged

Renew and/or replace the connection involved

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
