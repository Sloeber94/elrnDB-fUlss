Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Radio P020:

- it receives power supply (pin 12 connector D) from body computer node M001 (pin 1 connector D).

- is interfaced (pins 10 and 13 connector A), via the CAN line, with warning light and odometer control unit node M140 (pins 2 and 5), control panel E050 (pins 6 and 12), body computer node M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D) and multifunction screen node M172 (pins 2 and 4);

- it is connected (pins 3 and 9 connector A) to microphone P027 (pins 1 and 3 respectively);

- it supplies (pins 7 and 1 connector A) exterior fan N160 (pins 1 and 3 respectively);

- it is connected (pin 1 connector E and pin 1 connector F) to GPS/GSM aerial P110 (pin 1);

- it is connected (pins 7, 2, 1, 3, 8, 10, 4 connector B) to CD loader P075 (pins 1, 7, 3, 9, 10, 6, 5 respectively);

- it is connected (pins 1, 5 connector B and pin 3 connector D) to aerial and heated rear window control unit P022 (pins 3, 2, 1 respectively);

- it is connected (pins 9, 15 connector D and pin 6 connector A) to colour multifunction screen M172 (pins 1, 7, 2 respectively);

- it is connected (pins 4, 10, 11 connector A) to audio-video socket D055.

Multifunction colour screen M172 is also connected to audio-video socket D055 via pins 11 and 5.

The Connect radio P020 is connected, via connector C, to the various speakers located inside the vehicle:

- from pins 1, 5 it is connected to the speakers P048 (right rear tweeter) and P121 (right rear speaker);

- it is connected, from pins 2, 6, to the speakers P036 (speaker in right front door) and P046 (right front tweeter);

- it is connected, from pins 3, 7, to the speakers P035 (speaker in left front door) and P045 (left front tweeter);

- from pins 4, 8 it is connected to the speakers P047 (left rear tweeter) and P120 (left rear speaker on side panel);

Pins 2 and 8 of connector A are connected to speakers P035 and P045 also used for the hands-free function in the telephone mode.

The radio/GSM aerial P111 is connected to connector E of P020.

It is possible, via the steering wheel node M093, to control several of the radio functions using the radio controls on the steering wheel; this information is sent by the steering wheel node control unit M093 to the body computer node M001, via the CAN which connects the two control units.
