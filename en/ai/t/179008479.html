Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Brake pedal switch I030 is supplied, at pin 1, by maxifuse box B005, and is protected by fuse F31 located inside the supplementary fuse box B098.

The brake light command from pin 2 of brake pedal switch I030 is sent to pin 4 of body computer node M001.

When the pedal is pressed, the body computer node supplies:

- from pin 15 of connector L, pin 2 of left tail-light F030;

- from pin 16 of connector L, pin 2 of right tail-light F031;

- from pin 14 of connector L, pin 1 of extra tail-light (third brake light) F040;
