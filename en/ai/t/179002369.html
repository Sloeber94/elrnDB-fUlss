Operational Description

All electrical systems and circuits are supplied by battery A001.

The power supply to the various devices is managed by engine bay junction units B001 and body computer node M001.

Several MAXIFUSES are located in the body computer node M001:

  • MF04 and MF05 which supply the engine compartment junction unit B001, at pins 1 and 2 of connector E;
  • MF07 which supplies the ignition switch H001;
  • MF01 and MF06 which supply the engine fans;See E5020 ENGINE COOLING
  • MF02 and MF03 which supply the ABS/ESP control unit;See E7023 ESP
  • MF08 which supplies the climate control system control unit.See E5020 ENGINE COOLING

Some of the optional system power supply lines are protected by MAXIFUSES grouped together in a casing B005 located next to the battery and directly connected to it:

  • MF1 and MF2 supply the sliding side door node M171.(Wiring Diagram ) E3095 SLIDING SIDE DOORS
  • MF3 supplies the additional heater control unit.(Wiring Diagram ) E6015 ADDITIONAL HEATER

Other fuses are grouped together in an additional fuse box B098:

  • F32 and F33 supply the seat adjustment circuits.(Wiring Diagram ) E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS
  • F34 and F35 supply the sun roof adjustment circuits;See E3060 SUN ROOF
  • F36 and F37 supply the seat heating circuits;(Wiring Diagram ) E3075 HEATED SEATS
  • F39 and F40 supply the current socket circuits;See E3070 CIGAR LIGHTER / CURRENT SOCKET
  • F38 supplies the child lock circuit.See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING

The ignition switch H001 receives a direct battery supply at pin 1 connector A for MAXIFUSE MF07 of the body computer M001.

The ignition-operated supply (key in position M) is provided:

  • at pins 1 and 2 of connector B; at the engine compartment junction unit B001, at pins 1 and 2 of connector C;
  • from pin 2 connector A to the body computer node M001, pin 9 of connector C;
  • from the same pin 2 connector A to the brake pedal switch I030.

The starter motor A020 is supplied from pin 3 connector A of H001 (key in position D).

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Engine bay junction unit B001 and body computer node M001 both contain fuses and relays that protect the supply lines to connected devices.
