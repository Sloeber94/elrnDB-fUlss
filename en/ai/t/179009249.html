Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The radiator and air conditioner condenser cooling system consists of two electric fans: there are two different speeds depending on the temperature of the engine coolant or the pressure of the air conditioning refrigerant.

The fans are activated by three relays connected to one another: depending on the temperature and pressure sensor signals, the connections are series or parallel and produce the speed levels mentioned above
