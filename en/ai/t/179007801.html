Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The side lights come on when the ring nut on the steering column switch unit left lever is turned to the first position (steering wheel node).

The side lights control function is activated through the enablement signals with the key in position M and the command from the steering column switch unit: all the side lights and also the number plate lights and numerous other interior lights for lighting the passenger compartment, instruments and controls are activated together with the side lights (these are described and explained in the wiring diagrams for the relevant components).

Activation/deactivation information is sent via the CAN network: in this way, the side lights warning light in the instrument panel is also activated. The instrument panel also activates the nightime lighting.

The side lights can also be activated automatically, using the AUTO button on the steering column switch unit: this function is achieved by means of an infrared dusk sensor located in the windscreen.

Activation and deactivation is accompanied by an auditory warning signal.

When the device is switched on, the lights come on automatically even if the windscreen wiper is operated.

When the device is switched on, even if the windscreen wiper is operated, the follow me home function comes on making it possible to keep the dipped headlamps and the side lights on even after the engine has been switched off (key in position S) for a period of 45 seconds.

The side lights warning light in the instrument panel signals that the lights have remained on. The lights are activated by operating the light flasher stalk within 2 minutes of switching the engine off.
