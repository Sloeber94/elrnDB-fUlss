Operational Description

On versions which have additional rear fans N146 and N147, pin 1 receives a power supply from the engine compartment junction unit B001, pin 8 connector F, from a line protected by fuse F18 and governed by a specific relay, both housed inside the junction unit. They are earthed at pin 3.

The electric motors N146 and N147 are operated by control unit M070, pin 4,5 connector A.

The command is supplied by selectors H125 and H126: they receive a power supply for switching on the built-in warning light, at pin 1, from the body computer node M001, pin 12 connector F, through the line protected by fuse F12 and governed by a specific relay, located in the junction unit.

They are supplied at pin 5 by control unit M070, pin 7 connector A. From pin 6, they send control signals for the rear fans to control unit M070, pin 6, 13 connector A.

The sensor which measures the outside temperature is located on the passenger door mirror P061: the sensor is connected to the control unit on the door M066 and from there it sends information to control unit M070 via the CAN.

Control unit M070 communicates other information (heated rear windscreen on, air conditioning on) from pins 1, 2, 3 to the instrument panel E050 to pins 5, 9 and 2 and from there to the various displays.

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

The diesel version signal is sent to pin 10 of the instrument panel E050 from pin 18 connector A of M070.

Climate control unit M070 receives a supply direct from the battery at pin 11, connector A from body computer node M001, pin 7 connector D from the line protected by fuse F12 and managed by a specific relay, contained inside the control unit.

It receives an ingition-operated power supply at pin 12 connector A, from the body computer node M001, from pin 6 connector F, from a line protected by fuse F18.

Pin 16 of connector A is earthed

Control unit M070 communicates from pins 8, 14, 15 via the CAN with the body computer node M001 at pins 6, 8, 10 connector D and with instrument panel E050 at pins 23, 17, 13 (for more details

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Passenger compartment air fan N085 is controlled by selector unit H081. The unit contains an internal control unit that controls passenger compartment speeds according to the speed set on the appropriate selector.

The relay J035 supplies the selector unit H081, at pin B connector A, on the go ahead of the body computer node M001, from pin 40 connector M, providing a direct battery supply at the selector H091, via the line protected by fuse MF08 for the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 8 connector G).

The same power is also suppiled to passenger compartment air temperature sensor K089, pin 1.

Pin 3 of sensor K089 and pin A connector A of H081 are connected to earth.

The selector H081, connector B, is then operated by the control unit M070, from pins 2 and 3 of connector B, according to the strategy for implementing the management of the internal ventilation.

Sensor K089 is connected, from pins 4, 2, 6 to the climate control system control unit M070 at pins 17, 18, 16 connector B, respectively, informing the control unit of the value of the temperature measured inside the passenger compartment.

Climate control unit M070 receives data from the body computer node M001 via the CAN, from solar sensor K090. The latter is connected to body computer node M001 at pins 18 and 8 connector L.

Pins 12, 14, 15 connector B of control unti M070 receive signals from the mixed air temperature sensors, i.e. lower K087 and upper K088.

Pins 6, 7, and 8 connector B of electronic control unit M070 supply the electric motors for recirculation N082, left mixing N078, right mixing N079 and distribution N080 at pins 1. Pins 3, 4, 5, 6 connector C of control unit M070 receive the angular opening position of the internal air intake flaps.
