Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Engine management control unit M010 controls and governs the entire electronic ignition and injection system and consequently the FAP system as well.

The control unit M010 communicates via the CAN line (pins A3 and A4 connector A) with the body computernode M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D).

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

The diesel additive control unit M180 manages the addition of additive into the fuel.

The control unit M180 is connected (pins 6 and 7), via the CAN line, to the Body computer node M001 (pins 35 and 37 connector E) which (pin 12 connector B) also provides the power supply (pin 1).

The same control unit M180, is connected (pins 9 and 100 to the diesel additive pump N176 (pins 3 and 4 respectively) and (pins 16 and 15) to the diesel additive level sensor K186 (pins 1 and 2 respectively).

The main injection relay, inside the engine compartment junction unit B001, controls the entire system: it is energized by a control signal coming from pin H2 of connector B of the control unit M010.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Once energized, it provides a power supply (pin 24 connector B of B001) to the injection pump third piston deactivating solenoid valve L005 (pin 2) and intake air heating solenoid valve L097 (pin 1) through a line protected by fuse F10.

During the artificial regeneration, the injection pump third piston deactivation solenoid valve L005 controls the high pressure pump third piston to ensure the correct fuel flow rate required for post-injection: pin 1 is connected to pin A4 of connector B of the engine management control unit M010.

The following are also connected to the same control unit M010 (connector B):

- the intake air heating solenoid valve L097 which is controlled at pin 2 from pin B3;

- the temperature sensor downstream of the catalyzer K188 with pins 1 and 2 at pins F3 and E3 respectively;

- the differential pressure sensor K187, with pins 1, 2 and 3 at pins D3, M3 and H2 respectively.
