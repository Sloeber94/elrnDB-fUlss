Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

1. Undo and remove the fuel filler pipe cap.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the fuel flap (1b) from the housing.

2. Working on the throttle (2a), separate the flap from the opening cable (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolt (1a) and remove the filler pipe housing seal (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel filler pipe.

2. Disconnect the band (2a) and remove the fuel filler pipe (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the filler pipe in the special housing and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the seal in the filler housing and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Connect the flap opening cable to the actual flap and tighten the fuel flap fixing bolts to the bodyshell.

- Fit the fuel filler pipe cap.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.
