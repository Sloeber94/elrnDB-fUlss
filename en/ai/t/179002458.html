Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The following diagram shows the various earths on the car. The associated circuits are shown for each earth.

This makes it easy to identify the earthed circuits on an individual line: faults and failures are therefore easier to locate in the case of problems that affect more than one function: for example, oxidation of an earth may put several circuits and numerous functions out of service simultaneously.

some earths belonging to the same wiring are interconnected via various welds in the wiring. This allows all the electrical components on the same wiring to be earthed even if a particular earth point cannot allow effective contact with the vehicle body.
