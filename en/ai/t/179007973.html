Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: front wing detached, front door detached, sliding side door detached, sliding door lower guide detached, lower rear wing detached, front and rear door entry seals detached

The procedure in question involves the complementary operation of partly stripping the lower parts of the front, centre and rear pillars in addition to partly stripping the floor panel.
