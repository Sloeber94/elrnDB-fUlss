K117 INSTRUMENT STOP SIGNAL

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The STOP signal indicates the simultaneous presence of a fault that is normally signalled by the specific warning lights

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The coming on of the warning light depends on the information received by the Body Computer Node and it is displayed in the Milometer Warning Lights Node located on the steering column.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
