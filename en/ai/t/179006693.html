The dipped headlamps must already be on in order to activate the Fog Lights. .

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fog lights are controlled by the Steering Wheel Controls Node NVO, managed by the Body Computer Node BSI and activated by the Engine Compartment Junction Unit Node BSM

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

See table I20 FOG LIGHTS NOT WORKING

1

FOG LIGHTS ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • Protective fuse(s) circuit.
  • Condition of the bulbs and associated bulb holder.
  • Earth for bulbs and/or bulb holder.
  • Wiring between junction unit and bulbs.

See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

Move on to Step

1. Protective fuse/s blown.

1. Replace the component involved (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

2. Wiring defective.

2. Repair wiring.

3. Bulbs and/or bulb holder damaged.

3. Repair or replace the bulb and/or front light cluster.

4. Bulb holder earth faulty.

4. Renew bulb holder earth.

2

ALL OK

Check that the fault is no longer present
