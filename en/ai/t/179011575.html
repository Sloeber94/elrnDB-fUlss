Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is wired for the installation of a radio system.

This preparation includes:

- radio supply leads;

- front power speakers and separate tweeters;

- rear power speakers and separate tweeters;

- cables connecting the radio with the speakers;

- an aerial at the back mounted on the roof and connected by means of a coaxial cable to the radio.

The radio circuit is supplied both with the key in position M and it receives a direct supply from the battery (which allows tuning, safety codes, etc. to be programmed).

The main radio controls are duplicated on the steering wheel allowing the driver to operate the radio without taking their hands off the steering wheel.

To all intents the radio is a CAN node and is connected to the body computer, the instrument panel and the multifuction screen where information relating to the radio is displayed.

A special CD cartridge allows up to 6 CDs to be loaded which are then selected through the radio controls. The CD changer is located in the dashboard console.

The following is also available, depending on the trim level:

- RADIO with CD player, described in this section;

- CONNECT Nav" device (with navigator with pictograms);

See E4091 CONNECT

- CONNECT Nav +" device (with navigator with maps).

See E4090 NAVIGATOR

All the details on the specifications and the operation of the various models are contained in the "OWNER'S HANDBOOK".
