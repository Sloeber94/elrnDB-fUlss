Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water infiltration into the front part of the passenger compartment is noticed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK ABSENCE OF WINDSCREEN INFILTRATIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for windscreen infiltrations using water, soap and compressed air

Move on to Step

Small points where sealant is missing

Restore sealant working from inside the vehicle.

2

CHECK TANK DRAINAGE (SHROUD)

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for deposits

Move on to Step

Deposits in drainage

Clean drainages and correct fitting position if necessary

3

CHECK LUGGAGE RACK ATTACHMENT AREA

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check sealant and correct fitting of luggage rack attachment

Move on to Step

Roof/side panel join incorrect, unsealed

Restore sealant

4

CHECK WING FRONT PANEL AREA

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for presence of central plug under upper wing cover.Check that the door service cable sleeve is correctly fitted.

  • correct door/compartment alignment
  • correct door adherence

Move on to Step

Plug under cover and cable sleeve incorrectly fitted

Refit correctly

5

CHECK CORRECT DOOR ALIGNMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • correct door/compartment alignment
  • correct adherence of door to door compartment seal
  • absence of door compartment seal kinks/breaks

Move on to Step

Door is misaligned and does not adhere to the compartment/seal

Re-align correctly

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
