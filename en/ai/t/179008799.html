Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The opening of the front doors is signalled via the switch incorporated in the locks N050 and N051. When the door is opened, the (normally closed type) switch provides an earth signal at pin 4 (left front door) and pin 5 (right front door) for connector L of the body computer node M001.

Having received the signal, the body computer node M001 sends the command to the courtesy lights in the front doors G051 and G052, from pin 7 connector I, with a supply coming from the line protected by fuse F07 of M001.
