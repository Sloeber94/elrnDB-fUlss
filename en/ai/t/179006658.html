Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The siren is connected to the Alarm NodeThe alarm node is operated and controlled by the Body Computer Node via the VAN CAR2 network. If there is a fault, the Body Computer Node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction.The networks are on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks.
