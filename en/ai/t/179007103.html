Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector (1a) and move the hose (1b) aside.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the exhaust air injection system cut out valve.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the cut out valve and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the rapid connector for the hose removed previously.

- Fit the coil cover and renew the fastenings.
