Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the switch in the control panel is pressed, the heated rear windscreen and door mirrors defrosting is not working.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The defrosting of the rearscreen and the door mirrors is activated through the same switch in the control panel. Having received the defrosting command, the body computer node sends the signal, via the VAN CONFORT network, to the door nodes, which manage the defrosting of the door mirrors, whilst the defrosting of the rearscreen is managed directly.The networks go on stand-by 30 mins after the engine has been switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
