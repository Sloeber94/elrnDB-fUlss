Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The Body computer node M001 supplies the driver's door control unit M066 and the passenger door control unit M067, both at pin 1 of connector A from pin 9 of connector F.

The command is supplied from the Body computer node M001 (pins 36, 38 connector I for the driver's side and pin 38, 40 connector L for the passenger side) to the door control units M066 and M067 (pins 19, 20 connector A) via the CAN.

The control units M066 and M067 operate the electric motors for the door mirrors P060 and P061 directly.

The command for the movement of the mirrors is given by unit H051 where the three specific switches are located: one for selecting the (left or right) door mirror, one for adjusting the mirror and one for folding the mirror.

The door control unit M066 and the switch unit H051 interact to carry out a series of operations:

- unit H051 is supplied at pin 2 of connector A from pin 16 of connector A at M066;

- the selection of the mirror is communicated from pin 3 of connector A of unit H051 to pin 13 of the same connector of control unit M066;

- the type of adjustment made is communicated from pin 1 of connector A of unit H051 to pin 14 of the same connector of control unit M066.

To control the right mirror as well, the control unit M066 communicates via the CAN with control unit M067.

Control units M066 and M067 control (pins 2, 1, 3 connector B) the electric movement motors for the rear view mirrors P060 and P061 (the same pins and connector) and (pins 2 and 3 connector C) the mirror folding electric motors (the same pins and connector). Lastly, mirrors P060 and P061 (pins 5, 4, 6, 1 connector C) communicate the feedback signals (same pins and connector) to the respective control units.

Each time reverse gear is engaged, the interior rear view mirror P065 is placed in the automatic adjustment mode position.

The reversing switch I020 (or the automatic transmission solenoid valve assembly L050) when engaged, sends an earth signal (pin 1 or A1) to the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pin 13 or pin 15 connector F). The latter communicates the event (pin 11 and 12 connector F) via the CAN line to the Body computer node M001 (pin 27 and 25 connector E respectively) which (pin 22 connector L) sends a power supply to the interior rear view mirror P065 (pin 3).

The mirror P065 can also be activated manually by pressing the switch housed inside the actual mirror. In this case, the mirror will receive a power supply at pin 2 from pin 6 of connector F of the Body computer node M001.

On versions with seats with a memory, the movement of the mirrors in any of the 4 positions memorized is produced using the information transmitted via the CAN by the seats/mirrors with memory control unit M076 (pin 3A and 3B connector A).
