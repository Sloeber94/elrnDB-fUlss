Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Description

The vehicle is equipped with two rear fog lights located in the rear light clusters.

The rear fog lamps are activated via the special ring nut control on the stalk unit (steering wheel node); when the lights are on, the warning light in the instrument panel comes on.

The switching on of the rear fog lamps is managed by the body computer: it is activated by:

  • enablement signal with key in position M;
  • control signal coming from the steering column switch unit ring nut;
  • side lights on signal.
  • headlamp signal (dipped or main beams), or fog lights on.

If one of the enablement signals fails, the ring nut must be turned again to switch on the rear fog lamps.
