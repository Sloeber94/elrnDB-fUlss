Refitting

This operation must be carried out implementing all measures to ensure cleanliness and following the dismantling and reassembly procedure scrupulously.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the solenoid valve assembly.

Refitting

Because the following procedure requires absolutely cleanliness, clean the area around the automatic transmission valve cover thoroughly.

- Position a container for collecting the oil contained in the solenoid valve unit.

1. Disconnect the electrical connections for the sequence solenoid valves gently using a suitable size screwdriver.

Do not undo any other bolts.
