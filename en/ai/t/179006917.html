1. Place the oil pump and balancing shaft assembly, together with the gaskets underneath, in position, checking that they are correctly centered on the pins, then partly tighten the bolts (1b) and remove the locking tool.

The gaskets between the crankcase and the balancing shaft assembly should not, under any circumstances, be lubricated or covered in grease, also check that they have a minimum thickness of 1.57 mm if they are being reused.
