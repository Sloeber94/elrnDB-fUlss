Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The courtesy light for the glove compartment G030 is activated when it is opened by means of a switch incorporated inside. In particular, the Body computer node M001 provides a power supply at pin 1 of the glove compartment courtesy light G030, from pin 4 of connector D.

The ashtray light G035 (pin 1) also receives a power supply from the Body computer node M001 (pin 7 connector D).

The opening of the tailgate is signalled by the switch I011: with the tailgate open, the switch (pin 1) sends an earth signal to the Body computer node M001 (pin 10 connector I).

The power suply for the rear courtesy light G025 (pin 1) is provided by the Body computer node M001 (pin 16 connector I).

Lastly, the sun visor lights G031 (driver's side) and G032 (passenger side) receive a power supply at pin 1 from pin 20 of connector I of the Body computer node M001, like the front courtesy light G010 (pin 2 connector A).

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS
