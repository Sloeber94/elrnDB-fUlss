Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

WATER PUMP

It is the centrifugal type with vanes secured to the crankcase and operated directly through the timing belt.

THERMOSTAT

Fitted to the rear of the cylinder head and performs the function of maintaining the engine at optimum temperature:

· with temperature < 83 ± 2°C the thermostat valve (closed) directs coolant directly toward the water pump.

· with the temperature > 83 ± 2°C the thermostatic valve starts to open until it is completely open at 95°C, directing the flow of coolant toward the radiator.

The engine coolant temperature sensor connected to the Engine Management Node is fitted on the thermostat casing.
