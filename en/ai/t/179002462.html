Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The earths present on the vehicle, categorised according to their relationship with the various wires, are as follows.

Front wiring:

  • Left front earth C010 A
  • Left front earth C010 B
  • Right front earth C011 A
  • Right front earth C011 B
  • Right front earth C011 C
  • ABS front earth C012 A
  • ABS front earth C012 B
  • Air conditioning unit earth C016
  • Passenger side dashboard earth C020 C
  • Passenger side dashboard earth C020 E

Facia wiring:

  • Driver's dashboard earth C015 A
  • Passenger side dashboard earth C020 A
  • Centre facia earth C022

Rear wiring:

  • Passenger side dashboard earth C020 D
  • Left rear earth C030
  • Right rear earth C031 A
  • Right rear earth C031 B
  • Air Bag system earth C050
  • Earth on bodyshell for seats C051
  • Rain sensor earth C093

Tailgate wiring:

  • Right rear earth C031 C

Battery negative wiring:

  • Battery earth C001

Engine wiring:

  • Earth on engine C040 A
  • Earth on engine C040 B
