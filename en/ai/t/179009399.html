Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The compressor activation control is given by pressing the key housed on the climate control unit M070.

Control unit M070 (pin 10 connector A) receives power via a line protected by fuse F14 leaving body computer node M001 (pin 6 connector D) and communicates via the CAN line (pins 14 and 15 connector A) with instrument panel E050 (pins 6 and 12) and with body computer node M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D).

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Body computer M001, again via the CAN line, forwards the command to engine control unit M010 (pins A3 and A4 connector A), enables or disables compressor activation (pins F2, H2 and F4 connector A) from linear sensor K120 (pin 1, 2 and 3).

Compressor L020 is supplied (pins 1 and 2) from engine compartment junction unit B001 (pins 1 and 5 connector C), which is connected in turn (pins 12 and 11 connector F) via the CAN line to body computer node M001 (pins 25 and 27 connector C).

Demister sensor K086 is also connected (pins 1 and 2) to body computer node M001) (pins 17 and 16 connector E respectively): the signal contains information on the evaporator temperature, which is also sent to control unit M070.
