Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The command to switch on the side/number plate lights is supplied by the steering wheel node control unit M093 via the CAN line (pins 1 and 3 of connector A) to the body computer node M001 (at pins 5 and 3 of connector G respectively).

The steering wheel node control unit M093 is connected to earth C015 (connector A) via pin 4 of connector A and receives a power supply, at pin 5 of the same connector, from pin 11 of connector B of Body computer node M001.

Having received the command, the Body computer node M001 provides a power supply:

- to pin 1 of the right rear light cluster F031, from pin 12 (connector I);

- to pin 1 of the left rear light cluster F030, from pin 13 (connector I);

- to pin 1 of the number plate lights F050 and F051, from pin 18 (connector I).

In addition, the Body computer node M001, acts at pins 12 and 11 (respectively) of connector F of the engine compartment junction unit B001 via the CAN line (pins 25 and 27 of connector E). The latter (via pins 11 and 12 of connector G) acts on pins 3 of the front right headlamp F011 and the front left headlamp F010 (respectively).

The switching on of this special warning light is managed by the milometer warning light node M140 which communicates, via the CAN (pins 2 and 5) with the instrument panel E050 (pins 6 and 12) and the body computer node M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D).

The body computer node M001 supplies the milometer warning lights node M140, at pin 4 and the instrument panel E050 at pin 13, via pin 6 of connector D.

The solar/dusk sensor K125, provides information, via pins 1, 2, 4, concerning the outside light, to the Body computer node M001, at pins 3, 24, 26 (respectively) of connector L.
