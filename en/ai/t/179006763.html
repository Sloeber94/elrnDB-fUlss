The sun roof (front/centre/rear) is noisy when opening/closing and/or whilst driving

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

WATER DRAINAGE SYSTEM CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the noise is only present when opening/closing the roof and not whilst driving with the roof completely closed;check that the roof panel is correctly fastened

Move on to Step

The noise is also present whilst driving with the roof closed and the roof panel is not fastened

Fasten the roof panel

2

TERMINAL ADJUSTMENT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the bolts fixing the motor are correctly tightened

Move on to Step

Tightening not correct

Tighten correctly

3

MOTOR TIGHTENING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

Fault persists, inner frame not correctly fastened

Detach the roof panel and fasten the inner frame correctly
