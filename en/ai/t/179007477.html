Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the fixing nut.

2. Place the mounting bracket for the pipe from the expansion tank to the EGR heat exchanger at the side.

3. Undo the retaining nuts.

4. Disconnect the pipes from the expansion valve.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Position the ordinary tools on the heater coolant supply and return pipes.

2. Open the retaining clips and disconnect the heater coolant supply and return pipes.

- Collect the coolant in a suitable container.

3. Undo the retaining bolt.

4. Remove the protection.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the condensation drainage pipe.

2. Undo the lower retaining bolt

3. Remove the air conditioning/heater casing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the air conditioning/heater casing.

- Tighten the lower fixing bolt.

- Connect the condensation drainage pipe.

- Fit the protection and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Connect the heater coolant supply and return pipes and close the retaining clips.

- Remove the ordinary tools positioned previously.

- Connect the pipes to the expansion valve.

- Tighten the retaining nuts.

- Position the mounting bracket for the pipe from the expansion tank to the EGR heat exchanger.

- Tighten the fixing nut.
