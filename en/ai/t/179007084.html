1076A40 EXHAUST PIPE HEAT SHIELD - R R

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

See in Op. 1076B20

1. Using a drill fitted with a suitable size helical point, drill out and remove the fastening rivets.

2. Remove the front section of the exhaust pipe heat shields.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the nut (1a) and, using a drill fitted with a suitable size helical point, drill out and remove the fastening rivets (1b).

2. Remove the rear section of the exhaust pipe heat shield.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the rear heat shield in its housing and secure it using the fixing nut.

- Refit the rivets using a riveter.

- Fit the front shield and refit the rivets using a riveter.

See in Op. 1076B20

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods
