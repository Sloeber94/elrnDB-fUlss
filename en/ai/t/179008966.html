Operational Description

The electric motors for the front windows are incorporated in the door nodes M066 and M067.

The door nodes M066 and M067 are supplied by the body computer node at pin 16 connector from the line protected by fuse F09.

The door nodes M066 and M067 receive a supply via the CAN (pin 1 connector A) from the line protected by fuse F04 of the body computer M001.

The body computer node M001 also communicates with the door nodes M066 and M067 via the CAN, namely from pins 21, 23 and 24, 26, connector L to pins 14, 3 connector A of the driver's door node M066 and the passenger door node M067.

Information on the current position of the windows and the opening/closing commands through the operation of the remote control is provided via the CAN.

It is possible to open/close the two front electric windows using the driver's side switch H051. The supply for the lighting is provided by the driver's door node M066 from pin 15 connector A, the earth from pin 9 connector A; the control unit M066 receives the window activation signal at pins 4 and 5 of connector A.

The command for opening/closing the passenger side window also takes place through the switch H050; the supply for the lighting is provided by node M067 from pin 15 connector A which receives the window activation signal at pins 4 and 5 of connector A.

The door nodes M066 and M067 are connected and communicate with the body computer node M001, at pins 21, 23, 24, 26 of connector L
