Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle brake lights are located at the rear in the side light clusters and in the special central third brake light and they consist of 10 separate bulbs.

They are operated each time the brake pedal is pressed via the special switch on the actual pedal.

there is a second "redundant" switch on the brake pedal used, in particular, by the engine management control unit and the ABS control unit.The "basic" switch (I030) is the "normally open" type (when the pedal is pressed, the switch closes), whilst the "redundant" switch (I035) is the "normally closed" type (when the pedal is pressed, the switch contact opens): the system checks the consistency of the "pedal pressed" information in this way.
