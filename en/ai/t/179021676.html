Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the retaining band (1a) and disconnect the headlamp washer jet (1b) from the screen washer fluid supply pipe (1c).

2. Adjust the retaining clips and remove the headlamp washer jet.

- Carry out the same operations for remaining headlamp washer jet.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the front light cluster (1b).

- Disconnect the electrical connector for the front light cluster.

- Carry out the same operations for remaining front light cluster.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the light cluster in position and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the electrical connector for the front light cluster.

- Carry out the same operations for remaining front light cluster.

- Position the headlamp washer jet and secure it to the support.

- Connect the screen washer fluid supply pipe to the headlamp washer jet and secure it using a suitable band.

- Carry out the same operations for remaining headlamp washer jet.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
