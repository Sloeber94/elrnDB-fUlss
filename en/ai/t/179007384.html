Removing

Refitting

- Proceed as follows to remove the left rear shock absorber.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL R R

1. Position a hydraulic lift under the rear axle.

2. Loosen the upper (2a) and lower (2b) fixing bolts.

- Using the lift, release the fixing bolts and remove them.

3. Remove the shock absorber.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Proceed in the same way to remove the right rear shock absorber.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Proceed as follows to refit the left rear shock absorber.

- Position the shock absorber.

- Using the lift, fit the fixing bolts.

- Tighten the fixing bolts to the recommended torque.
