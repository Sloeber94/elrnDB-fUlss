Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is equipped with a climate control system for managing the temperature, air flow rate, distribution and recirculation.

The control unit manages the operation of the system by controlling:

  • air temperature at the vents;
  • air distribution at the vents;
  • fan speed (continuous variation);
  • compressor engagement (air refrigeration circuit);
  • air recirculation engagement.

The system controls are located on the outside of the container which houses the control unit: the following parameters/functions can be controlled manually:

  • fan speed;
  • distribution arrangement;
  • compressor enablement;
  • defrosting/demisting;
  • recirculation.
