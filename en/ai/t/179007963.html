Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: bumper removed, rear crossmember removed, tailgate open.

The procedure in question involves the complementary operation of replacing the end of the (left or right) rear half side member.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
