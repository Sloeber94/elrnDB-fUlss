Removing

Refitting

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7040A60 UNDER FACIA TRIM - R+R

1. Lift up the fastening spring (1a) and remove the pin (1b) complete with spacer (1c).

2. Remove the brake pedal.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the brake pedal.

- Fit the pin complete with spacer and secure it using the fastening spring.

Op. 7040A60 UNDER FACIA TRIM - R+R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.
