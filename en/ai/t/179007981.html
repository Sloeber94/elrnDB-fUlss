Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: windscreen removed, sun roof (if fitted) removed, roof lining removed, roof panel arch removed, fixed rear side window glass removed, tailgate removed, roof bar removed, sliding side doors removed, aerial removed, window bags removed, wiring released and moved aside.

The procedure in question involves the complementary operation of replacing the roof panel.

Refitting

Removing

- Position the front crossmember and the roof panel and fasten using self-locking clamps then, after having checked that the alignment and clearances are correct, remove the roof panel and proceed as described below.

- Using a spot welder, carry out the welding illustrated in the diagram then grind the welds using a disc grinder.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Check that the welded component is correctly positioned.

- Apply electro-galvanizing paint to the ground parts.

- Proceed with waxing the box sections.

When the operation is completed, refit the elements removed previously.
