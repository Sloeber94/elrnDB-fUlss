Removing

- Posiion the gear engagement flexible roads working via the passenger compartment.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the selector unit to the gearbox.

- Working via the engine compartment, pull the sensor and position the rapid connectors for the gear engagement flexible rods on the selector unit.

- Fasten the ends of the gear engagement flexible rods to the selector unit.

- Position the rubber retainer.
