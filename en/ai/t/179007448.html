5010C60 LH OR RH REAR AIR OUTLET -ONE- R R

Removing

Refitting

- To dismantle the left air outlet rear vent, proceed as described below.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7040H42 REAR PILLAR TRIM - R R

Op. 7040H50 REAR PANEL - R R

Op. 7040B22 LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT/LOAD PLATFORM MOULDING - R+R

Op. 7040H56 REAR SIDE PANEL FLAP (ONE) - R.R.

Op. 7040G30 BOOT LIGHT BULB HOLDER - R R

Op. 7040B12 LOAD COMPARTMENT/LOAD PLATFORM LEFT SIDE TRIM - R R

1. Undo the fastening button.

2. Remove the trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release and remove the rear air outlet vent.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Proceed in the same way to dismantle the right rear outlet air vent.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- To refit the left air outlet rear vent, proceed as described below.

- Position the rear outlet air vent and check that it is secured.

- Position the trim and secure it using the special fastening button.

Op. 7040B12 LOAD COMPARTMENT/LOAD PLATFORM LEFT SIDE TRIM - R R

Op. 7040G30 BOOT LIGHT BULB HOLDER - R R

Op. 7040H56 REAR SIDE PANEL FLAP (ONE) - R.R.

Op. 7040B22 LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT/LOAD PLATFORM MOULDING - R+R

Op. 7040H50 REAR PANEL - R R

Op. 7040H42 REAR PILLAR TRIM - R R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Proceed in the same way to refit the right rear outlet air vent.
