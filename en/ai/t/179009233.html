Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

- Disconnect the cable connector block from the front control unit.

See in Op. 7030B31 SUN ROOF BLIND (one), front, centre or rear - R.R. with roof panel lining removed

See in Op. 7030B27 SUN ROOF BLIND (one), front, centre or rear - R.R.

See in Op. 7030B73 Front, centre or rear SUN ROOF MOTOR (one) - R.R.

See in Op. 5505A66 Front, centre or rear sun roof (one) NODE (Digital network control unit) - R.R.

1. Undo the fixing bolts from inside the passenger compartment.

2. Extract the sun roof from the outside.

2. Remove the sun roof frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the sun roof in the housing.

- Fit the frame in place and tighten the fixing bolts from inside the passenger compartment.

See in Op. 5505A66 Front, centre or rear sun roof (one) NODE (Digital network control unit) - R.R.

See in Op. 7030B73 Front, centre or rear SUN ROOF MOTOR (one) - R.R.

See in Op. 7030B27 SUN ROOF BLIND (one), front, centre or rear - R.R.

See in Op. 7030B31 SUN ROOF BLIND (one), front, centre or rear - R.R. with roof panel lining removed

- Connect the cable connector block to the front control unit.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
