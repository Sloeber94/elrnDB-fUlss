Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the fuel pipes acting on the rapid connectors.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

3. Undo the fuel filter cover.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Lift up the top part of the filter (1a) and remove the filter element (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit a new fuel filter filter element.

- Tighten the fuel filter cover by hand.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Connect the fuel pipes disconnected previously.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
