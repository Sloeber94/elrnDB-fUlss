The engine seems to be running smoothly, but the fuel consumption is higher than ever before.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

N.B.: excessive consumption during the first few thousands of Km of the vehicle's life is considered normal.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually check for any leaks that could have caused the high consumption;

Move on to Step

Obvious oil leaks

Locate the leaks and eliminate them

1

CYLINDER HEAD OIL PENETRATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the head and check:

Move on to Step

Excessive clearance between the valve stem and valve guide and between the valve stem and oil seal

Replace the damaged/worn components < R1 >

2

ENGINE OIL PENETRATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the piston seals, in particular:

  • that they are not broken or damaged
  • that the seals are correctly fitted
  • that there is no seizing in the piston ring grooves

Move on to Step

1. Piston rings damaged or seized

1. Replace the pistons, gudgeon pins and rings

2. Piston rings not correctly fitted

2. Fit the piston rings correctly

3

CYLINDER BORE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • The condition of the surfaces and the dimensions (ovality/taper) of the cylinder bores;
  • Piston-bore fitting clearance;

Move on to Step

1. Bore surfaces do not conform

1. Regrind the cylinder bores

2. Dimensions outside of the tolerance

2. Regrind the cylinder bores

3. Fitting clearance not correct

3. Replace the pistons, gudgeon pins and piston rings following the fitting clearances

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
