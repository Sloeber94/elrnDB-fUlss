Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Engine management control unit M010 controls and governs the entire electronic ignition and injection system and consequently the FAP system as well.

Control unit M010 communicates via the CAN line (pins A3 and A4 of connector A) with body computer node M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D).

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Body computer M001 (pins 25 and 27 connector E) is also connected (via the CAN line) to engine compartment junction unit B001 (pins 11 and 12 connector F respectively).

Diesel additive control unit M180 manages the addition of additive to the fuel.

Control unit M180 is connected (pins 6 and 7) via the CAN line to body computer node M001 (pins 35 and 37 connector E), which (pin 12 connector B) also supplies it with power (pin 1).

Control unit M180 is also connected (pins 9 and 10) to diesel additive fuel pump N176 (pins 3 and 4 respectively) and (pins 16 and 15) to diesel additive level sensor K186 (pins 1 and 2 respectively).

The injector main relay inside engine compartment junction unit B001 controls the entire system: it is energized by a control signal coming from pin H2 of connector B of the control unit M010.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Once excited, it supplies power (pin 24 connector B of B001) to injection pump piston third deactivation solenoid L005 (pin 2) and intake air heating solenoid L097 (pin 1) via a line protected by fuse F10.

During artificial regeneration, injection pump piston third deactivation solenoid L005 controls the thirdhigh pressure pump piston to ensure the correct fuel flow required for post-injection. pin 1 is connected to pin A4 of connector B of engine control unit M010.

The following are also connected to control unit M010 (connector B):

- intake air heating solenoid L097, which is controlled at pin 2 by pin B3;

- temperature sensor downstream of catalytic converter K188, with pins 1 and 2 on pins F3 and E3 respectively;

- differential pressure sensor K187, with pins 1, 2 and 3 on pins D3, M3 and H2 respectively;

- Body computer node M001, with pin 8 of connector B on pin C3;

- heated water intake solenoid L006, with pin 2 on pin C2.

Pin 1 of L006 is connected to pin 3 of connector B of body computer node M001.
