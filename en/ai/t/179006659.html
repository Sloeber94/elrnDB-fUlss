Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the alarm is switched on using the remote control, the system is activated correctly but the volumetric sensors do not intervene.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The volumetric sensors are located in the front courtesy light and are connected to the Alarm Node.The volumetric sensors can be excluded by the customer using the special button.The networks are on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
