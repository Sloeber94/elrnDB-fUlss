Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 5010B18 CLIMATE CONTROL UNIT AIR INTAKE - R.R.

Op. 1048A10 AIR FILTER ASSEMBLY - R.R.

Raise the vehicle.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Position a hydraulic support under the gearbox and differential unit bellhousing.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the flexible mounting to the bracket.

2. Undo the nut fixing the flexible mounting to the rigid support.

3. Remove the flexible mounting from the housing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the flexible mounting in the housing in the engine.

Tighten the bolts and the nut fixing the flexible mounting to the bracket and to the rigid support to torque.
