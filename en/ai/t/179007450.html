Removing

Refitting

- Proceed as described below in order to remove the right upper mixed air temperature sensor.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7040H29 FRONT PILLAR LOWER COVER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Op. 7040A45 FACIA RH SIDE COVER - R R

Op. 7040G22 FACIA COMPARTMENT LIGHT SWITCH/BULB HOLDER - R+R

Op. 7040A31 FLAP ASSEMBLY - R R

- Proceed as described below in order to remove the left upper mixed air temperature sensor.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7040H29 FRONT PILLAR LOWER COVER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Op. 7040A44 SEAT INTERIOR GUARD - R R

Op. 7040A68 GRILLE FOR CENTRE DASHBOARD SPEAKER - R.R.

Op. 7040A47 ODDMENTS POCKET UNDER DASHBOARD - R.R.

Op. 7040A60 UNDER FACIA TRIM - R+R

Op. 5010B68 AIR DUCT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT BETWEEN TIMING CASING CONNECTOR AND FAN FOR REAR SEATS - R.R.

Op. 7040A65 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT BOX LOCK , ONE , R R

Op. 7040A89 SPEAKER GRILLE (ONE) LH OR RH ON INSTRUMENT FACIA - R R

-

- Proceed as described below in order to remove both the upper mixed air temperature sensors.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Proceed as described below in order to refit both the upper mixed air temperature sensors.

- Position the sensor and check that it is secured.

- Connect the electrical connection.

- Proceed as described below in order to refit the right upper mixed air temperature sensor.

Op. 7040A31 FLAP ASSEMBLY - R R

Op. 7040G22 FACIA COMPARTMENT LIGHT SWITCH/BULB HOLDER - R+R

Op. 7040A45 FACIA RH SIDE COVER - R R

Op. 7040H29 FRONT PILLAR LOWER COVER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Proceed as described below in order to refit the left upper mixed air temperature sensor.

Op. 7040A89 SPEAKER GRILLE (ONE) LH OR RH ON INSTRUMENT FACIA - R R

Op. 7040A65 PASSENGER COMPARTMENT BOX LOCK , ONE , R R

Op. 5010B68 AIR DUCT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT BETWEEN TIMING CASING CONNECTOR AND FAN FOR REAR SEATS - R.R.

Op. 7040A60 UNDER FACIA TRIM - R+R

Op. 7040A47 ODDMENTS POCKET UNDER DASHBOARD - R.R.

Op. 7040A68 GRILLE FOR CENTRE DASHBOARD SPEAKER - R.R.

Op. 7040A44 SEAT INTERIOR GUARD - R R

Op. 7040H29 FRONT PILLAR LOWER COVER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

- < R1 > [Left side]

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.
