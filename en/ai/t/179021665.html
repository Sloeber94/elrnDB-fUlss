Before removing the steering wheel, position the wheels as parallel as possible to the longitudinal axis of the vehicle.

The clock spring is fitted with a device that automatically stops it from rotating when it is removed. Avoid forcing it to rotate.

If the steering column switch unit is being reused, it is advisable, for extra safety, to secure the two clock spring plates to prevent accidental rotation.
