Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: Automatic Transmission and MY 2005

The battery A001 supplies the engine compartment junction unit B001 and the maxi-fuse box B006 at connector A. This last connection also reaches pin 30 of connector A of the starter motor M020 and from there pin B= of connector A of the alternator A010.

Pin 50 of connector B of A020 and D+ of connector B of A010 are connected to pins 2 and 3 of connector C of B001 respectively. Only the first of the two lines is protected by fuse F08.

The control unit M001 (pins 5 and 4 connector D) supplies the Body computer node M001 (pins 2 and 1 connector A) through two lines protected by fuses MF05 and MF04 (respectively).

The ignition switch H001 (pins 1, 2, 3) is connected to the Body computer node M001 (pin 4 connector G, pin 5 connector D and pin 6 connector G respectively).

The body computer M001 is still connected:

- from pin 6 of connector G, to control unit B001 (pin 9 connector F);

- from pin 6 of connector D, supplies, through a line protected by fuse F14, the instrument panel E050 (pin 13) and the milometer and warning lights control unit M140 (pin 4).

The Body computer node M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D), the instrument panel E050 (pins 6 and 12), the milometer and warning lights control unit M140 (pins 2 and 5) and the engine compartment junction unit B001 (pins 11 and 12 connector F) communicate with one another via the CAN line.

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

The anti-interference filter P087 is connected:

- at pin 1, with pin 2 of connector B of the maxi-fuse box B006;

- at pin 2, with connector A of earth C011 and with pin 19 of connector F of B001.

Lastly, pin A5 of the automatic transmission solenoid valve assembly L050 is connected to pin 15 of connector F of the engine compartment junction unit B001, whilst pin A4 is connected to connector A of the left front earth C010.
