Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle can be fitted with a homologated tow hook for caravans or trailers in general.

This device must meet current regulations and be installed in accordance with the instructions in the installation kit.

This installation kit includes the tow hook and also an electronic control unit for the exterior trailer lighting plus specific wiring for connecting the control unit to the vehicle tail-lights and a specific connection on the vehicle located in the boot on the right side.

Parking sensor operation is automatically inhibited if a trailer is connected.
