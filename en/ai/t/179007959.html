Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: interior trim removed, rear wing detached, rear wing frame detached.

The procedure in question involves the complementary operation of replacing the rear wing and the partial side member structure.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the rear foot and the partial rear section on the passenger compartment side and secure using self-locking clamps then, after having checked that the alignment is correct, dismantle the partial rear section on the passenger compartment side and proceed as described below.

- Proceed with brazing at the points shown in the diagram (F) then, using a spot welder, weld as illustrated in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Proceed with MIG welding in the areas (G) shown in the diagram and finish off in the remaining areas using a spot welder then grind the welds using a disc grinder.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Apply sealing compound along the lines illustrated in the diagram.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Grind the welds using a disc grinder.

- Check that the welded component is correctly positioned.

- Apply electro-galvanizing paint to the ground parts.

- Proceed with waxing the box sections.

When the operation is completed, refit the elements removed previously.
