Water infiltration is noted into the rear of the passenger compartment when raining or after washing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK FOR INFILTRATIONS THROUGH THE REAR SCREEN

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for infiltrations through the rear screen using water, soap and compressed air

Move on to Step

Small points where sealant is lacking

Restore the sealant working from inside the vehicle.

2

CHECK SEAL ROUND TAILGATE COMPARTMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check condition of seal and for kinks or deformations

Move on to Step

Seal with kinks and deformations

Replace seal

3

CHECK TAILGATE ALIGNMENT/SQUARENESS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the tailgate closed, check uninformity and alignment with the roof and tail-lights.Check the seal is correctly positioned around the edge of the tail-gate.

Move on to Step

The tail-gate is not square and is misaligned

Fit the tail-gate correctly

4

CHECK TAIL-LIGHTS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the seals are present and correctly positioned

Move on to Step

The seals are incorrectly positioned

Replace seals

5

CHECK ROOF RACK ATTACHMENT AREA

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the seal and that the roof rack attachment is correctly fitted

Move on to Step

Incorrect fit between roof and side panel, not sealed

Restore sealant

6

CHECK SWIVEL-OPENING REAR WINDOW

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for kinks and profiling of window sealsWith the window closed, check the seal adheres correctly to the window

Move on to Step

Seal not adhering and deformed

Replace seal

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
