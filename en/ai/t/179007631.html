Refitting

Before removing and refitting the switch for disabling the passgener Air Bag module the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it; (2) wait for 2 minutes before continuing; (3) remove the battery housing cover, disconnect the negative battery terminal and insulate the leads; (4) wait for 5 minutes before continuing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Op. 7040A44 SEAT INTERIOR GUARD - R R
