5580C18 AIR BAG MODULE - R+R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Before removing and refitting the Air Bag module the following preliminary operations must be carried out: (1) place the ignition key in the OFF position and extract it; (2) wait for 2 minutes before continuing; (3) remove the battery compartment cover, disconnect the negative battery terminal and insulate the leads; (4) wait for 5 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS (see assembly 5580C - AIR BAG SYSTEM).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Op. 7040A26 STEERING COLUMN TRIM - R+R

1. Release the retaining clips using a suitable tool.

2. Partly extract the Air Bag module.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the safety devices and disconnect the electrical connections.

2. Disconnect the earth.

3. Remove the Air Bag module.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Place the Air Bag module in the special cupboard, resting it on a free surface with the bag facing upwards.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Follow these safety precautions.

Handle the Air Bag module very carefully. Make sure that the battery is disconnected.

- If a new module is being installed, place the labels in position. Remove the removable part of the adhesive label from the new module and place it in a special file containing the vehicle details (model, registration date, chassis no.) and the serial number of the old module. The file should be kept for future checks. A new adhesive plate comes together with the module and should be stuck on top of the existing one. Beforehand, however, it should be perforated at the month and 10 years after the year in which the module is being fitted (e.g. 2010 for 2000).

- Connect the earth.

- Connect the electrical connections and secure them using the special safety devices.

- Place the Air Bag module in position and check that it is properly secured.

- Check the operation of the system using the Examiner or other diagnostic equipment.
