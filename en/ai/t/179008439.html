Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Operational Description

The brake lights command comes from the brake pedal switch I030, connected to the body computer node M001 at pin 2 of connector B and is supplied directly by the ignition switch H001.

When the pedal is pressed, supplies are sent to the left rear light cluster F030, from pin 1 of connector H of the body computer node M001, via the line for fuse F05 and to the right rear light cluster F031 and the third brake light F040, from pin 10 of connector H of M001, via the line for fuse F17.
