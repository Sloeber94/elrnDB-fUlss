Op. 1016A42 LEFT HAND CYLINDER HEAD IGNITION COIL COVER - R+R

Op. 7055B54 UNDER-ENGINE GUARD - R R

- Protect the radiator using stiff card.

1. Undo retaining bolts (1a), disconnect retaining bands (1b) and move air conditioning system pipe (1c).

2. Undo retaining bolts and move the front brackets securing the engine.

3. Undo the fixing bolts (3a) and remove the heat shield (3b) from the exhaust manifold, left side.

Applies to: engines: 3.0 24V V6; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the heat shield on the left exhaust manifold and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Position the front engine mounting brackets and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Position the air conditioning system pipe and tighten the bolts fixing the mounting bands.

- remove the protection from the radiator.
