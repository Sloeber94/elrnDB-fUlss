Operational Description

The electric adjustment of the front seats is operated by specific switches, housed in the seats, H066 and H067 and controlled by two specific relays J139 and J140 also housed in the seats.

The body computer node M001 provides the CAN supply, via two specific relays, housed inside and the line protected by fuse F01, to the relay J139 and J140 coils at pin 1 which is thereby energized. The relays receive a power supply, at pin 3, from the lines protected by fuses F32 and F36, located in the additional fuse box B098, which is then sent from pin 5 to the switches H066 and H067, pin 3; pin 4 is earthed.

The switches H066 and H067 operate the electric motors, from pins 5 and 6, for adjusting the position of the backrest: motors N132 and N135, respectively. The motors for adjusting the lengthwise position of the seat, N127 and N130, are operated from pins 1 and 2.
