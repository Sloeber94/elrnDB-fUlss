Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 7065C30 INTERIOR L/H OR R/H GRAB HANDLE (ONE) - R+R

- [Access to rear seats]

1. Remove the cover.

2. Undo the retaining bolt.

3. Releaset he centre pillar trim upper element and place it at the side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Release the cover.

3. Partly extract the centre pillar trim lower element.

4. Disconnect the electrical connections.

- Place the centre pillar trim lower element at the side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolt.

2. Release the seat belt lower fastening.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Release the centre pillar trim lower element from the lower seat belt fastening and remove it.

- Release the centre pillar trim upper element from the lower seat belt fastening and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the lower seat belt fastening inside the special housing in the centre pillar trim upper element.

- Place the lower seat belt fastening in position and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Connect the electrical connections.

- Place the centre pillar trim lower element in position.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.

- Position the cover.

- Place the centre pillar trim upper element in position.

- Tighten the fixing bolt.

- Position the cover.

Op. 7065C30 INTERIOR L/H OR R/H GRAB HANDLE (ONE) - R+R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
