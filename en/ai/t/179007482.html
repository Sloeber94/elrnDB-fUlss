Op. 5010B62 PURIFYING FILTER (ANTIPOLLEN) FILTER ELEMENT - REPLACE

1. Disconnect the electrical connections.

2. Undo the retaining bolts.

- Release the wiring.

3. Release and remove the air conditioning fan.
