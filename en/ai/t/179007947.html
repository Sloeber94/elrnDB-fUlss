Removing

Refitting

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Using a suitable tool, extract the front courtesy light (1a) and disconnect the electrical connection (1b) for the sun visor.

2. Undo the retaining bolts.

- Connect a sensor to the electrical wiring.

3. Remove the sun visor releasing the wiring from the roof lining.

4. Undo the retaining bolt.

5. Remove the visor attachment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the sun visor attachment and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Using the sensor connected previously, position the sun visor wiring on the roof lining.

- Position the sun visor and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection and place the front courtesy light in position.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.
