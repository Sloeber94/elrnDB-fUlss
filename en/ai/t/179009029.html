Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The current socket P050 receives a power supply (pin 2) from the Body computer node M001 (pin 1 connector F).

The light for the socket P050 (pin 1) is also supplied by the Body computer node M001 (pin 7 connector D).

The current socket P050 is earthed at pin 3.

Sockets P051 and P052 (pin 2) receive a supply from the lines protected by fuses F40 and F39, respectively, located in the additional fuse box B098 (pins G40 and G39 connector B respectively).

The sockets P051 and P052 are earthed at pin 3.

Sockets P051 and P052 are not lit up.
