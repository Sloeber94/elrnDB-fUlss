3

AERIAL CONNECTIONS AND WIRING CHECK.

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Extract the CONNECT from the dashboard using the special extractors and check that:

- The GPS aerial connector is correctly connected.

- The aerial body is securely fastened to the roof.

- There is electrical continuity for the two aerial lead poles.

Move on to Step 4.

The aerial lead connector is slack and/or disconnected.

Fit the connector in the special gauge.

The aerial body is loose and not securely attached to the roof.

Restore the correct torque tightening.

The lead is broken in at least one place.

Replace the aerial lead.
