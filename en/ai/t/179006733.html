Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The climate control works properly and sends air to both the left and right sides of the passenger compartments according to the pre-set flows, but this air does not ensure the heating required.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the (Examiner or other) diagnostic equipment connected, check the following:

  • that there are no faulty control units;
  • no control unit with faulty CAN line

Move on to Step

1. Presence of faults

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. Climate control system control unit error

Move on to Step

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the diagnostic equipment (Examiner or other instruments) connected to the diagnostic socket, check:

  • that there are no faults in the climate control node (NCL)

Move on to Step

1. Error detected in the control unit

1. Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2. No communication with the control unit

2. Restore the connection between the climate control system control unit and the diagnostic connector
