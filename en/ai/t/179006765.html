Applies to: all engines; all production periods

There is noise from the area of the sun roof (front/centre/rear) whilst driving;

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

SEAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check (with the roof closed):

  • that there is no damage to the seal
  • that the seal is fitted correctly
  • that the seal is suitably compressed

Move on to Step

The seal is not suitably compressed

Position the seal, if necessary, replace it if it is distorted

2

WEATHERSTRIP CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the weatherstrip is correctly positioned

Move on to Step

The seal is unstuck and not correctly positioned

Replace the seal

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the roof moves evenly and in a straight line by opening/closing it several times

Move on to Step

The movement of the roof is not even

Align the guides

4

ALIGNMENT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there is no uneveness between the sun roof outer frame and the roof panel

Move on to Step

The roof outer frame is not correctly matched with the roof panel

"Adjust" the sun roof outer frfame

5

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
