K28 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE SIGNALLED BY INSTRUMENT TOO LOW

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The message for the engine coolant temperature gauge is produced by the CCM Engine Management Node, converted by the Body Computer Node and then displayed in the by the Instrument Panel Node in the instrument in the centre of the dashboard.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
