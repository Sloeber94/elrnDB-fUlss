Operational Description

The command to switch on the side/no. plate lights is supplied by the steering wheel node control unit M093 to the body computer node M001. The signal travels via the CAN which connects the two control units.

The steering wheel node control unit M093 receives a supply (pin 5 connector A) through the line protected by fuse F11, located in the body computer node M001; it receives a supply via the CAN (pin 6 connector A) from the line protected by fuse F16, located in the body computer node M001.

On receiving the command, the body computer node M001 energizes the relay coil thereby providing a power supply:

  • for the left front light cluster F010 and the left rear light cluster F030, via the line protected by fuse F22;
  • for the right front light cluster F001, the right rear light cluster F031 and the no. plate lights F050 and F051, via the line protected by fuse F12.

The warning light is switched on by the milometer warning lights node M140 which communicates with the instrument panel E050 and the body computer node M001 via the CAN.

The instrument panel E050 and the milometer warning lights node M140 receive a supply via the CAN (pin 13 of E50 and pin 4 of M140) through the line protected by fuse F24 of the body computer M001.

The solar/dusk sensor K90 is connected to the body computer node M001, pins 6, 7 and 8 of connector L, providing information on the exterior light.
