Operational Description

The command to switch on the rearscreen wiper and the rearscreen washer is supplied by the steering wheel node control unit M093 to the body computer node M001. The signal travels via the CAN which connects the two control units.

The steering wheel node control unit M093 receives a supply (pin 5 connector A) through the line protected by fuse F11, located in the body computer node M001; it receives a supply via the CAN (pin 6 connector A) from the line protected by fuse F16, located in the body computer node M001.

The body computer node M001 informs the engine compartment junction unit B001, via the CAN, of the request to activate the rearscreen wiper and/or rearscreen washer.

The steering wheel node control unit M093 receives a supply (pin 5 connector A) from the line protected by fuse

The electric motor N016 is supplied by the body computer node M001 from pin 5 connector H by the line protected by fuse F02 and is operated by a specific relay both housed in the body computer node.

When the body computer node receives a request to switch on the rearscreen wiper, the electric motor is supplied at pin 1; when the end of travel position is reached, the switch inside the motor sends a signal from pin 2 to the body computer node M001, pin 9 connector I.

The command for the automatic activation when reverse gear is engaged arrives from the reversing switch I020 [EL2022] which sends it to pin 5 connector B of the body computer node M001 with the supply provided by the engine compartment junction unit B001 through the line protected by fuse F01.

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

The electric motor N022 carries out a dual windscreen and rearscreen washer function depending on the input of the motor power supply. For the rearscreen washer function, the supply, protected by fuse F14, is provided by pin 11 connector F of the engine compartment junction unit B001 at the electric motor N022, whilst the earth is sent from pin 14.

In addition to being protected by a specific fuse, the power supply provided is operated by a special relay also housed in the engine compartment junction unit.
