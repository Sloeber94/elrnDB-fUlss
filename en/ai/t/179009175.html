Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Battery A001supplies engine compartment junction unit B001 at connector A and maxifuse box B006. The fuse box connection is continued to pin 30 of connector A of starter motor A020 andt then on to pin B+ of connector A of alternator A010.

Pins 50 of connector B of A020 and D+ of connector B of A010 are connected to pins 2 and 3 of connector C of B001 respectively. Only the first of the two lines is protected by fuse F08.

Control unit B001 (pins 5 and 4 of connector D)supplies body computer node M001 (pins 2 and 1 of connector A) by two lines protected by fuses MF05 and MF04 (respectively).

Ignition switch H001 (pins 1, 2 and 3) is connected to body computer node M001 (pin 4 connector G, pin 5 connector D and pin 6 connector G respectively).

Body computer M001 is stil connected:

- by pin 6 of connector G to control unit B001 (pin 9 connector 'F);

- it supplies, from pin 6 of connector D, instrument panel E050 (pin 13) and warning light and odometer control unit M140 (pin 4) by a line protected by fuse F14.

Body computer M001 (pins 8 and 10 connector D), instrument panel E050 (pins 6 and 12), warning light and odometer control unit M140 (pins 2 and 5) and engine compartment junction unit B001 (pins 11 and 12 connector F) intercommunicate via the CAN line.

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Interference filter P087 is connected:

- at pin 1, to pin 2 of connector B of maxifuse box B006;

- at pin 2, with connector A of earth C011 and with pin 19 of connector F of B001.
