Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The climate control system will not work when the controls are operated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The climate control only works with the engine running.The nodes involved in the operation of the climate control are: NCL - NBC - NDVM - NCMThe networks go on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
