Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The electrical system is designed to increase the car's safety level and prevent the possibility of fires and other consequences of electrical malfunctions. State-of-the-art connectors have been introduced to rule out the possibility of many connection defects.

Power is distributed via the junction units and/or fuses boxes. These are connected to control elements (relays and static actuators) to ensure maximum electrical protection and minimum complexity.

The main safety solutions adoptd are as follows:

- power supplies protected by maxifuses to cut off the electrical system, in the case of short circuit; they are positioned just downstream of the battery;

- the layout of the entire system is designed to reduce damage in the case of a failure or accident;

- the routing of the actual cables is designed to reduce noise due to vibrations or incorrect positioning;

- all wires offer high resistance to abrasion;

- the wires in the engine compartment are resistant to high temperatures and protected with outer casings or piping;

- the main connections come with secondary lock terminals and lever locks to prevent terminal deformation.

All systems and electrical equipment are supplied by the battery at a voltage of 12 [V].

The battery is recharged by the alternator during engine operation

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

Some of the supply lines are protected by MAXIFUSES. These fuses are grouped together in a box located next to the battery and are connected directly to it.

Other fuses are grouped together in an additional fuse box, located near the MAXIFUSES.

The other fuses and the various relay feeds are located in the engine compartment junction unit and directly inside the body computer.

Some circuits receive a continuous power supply even with the vehicle stationary and the key switched off.

Other circuits are supplied by turning the ignition key to various positions:

- at the first turn, the key is in position M and the numerous "ignition-controlled" circuits are supplied;

- at the second turn (position D) the starter motor is supplied.

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

The lines that distribute power to the various appliances are represented by wiring diagrams for the various functions and systems. This general diagram shows all the lines as they leave the battery and maxifuse box. Refer to specific diagrams for more detail.
