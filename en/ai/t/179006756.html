Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Stiffness/noise detected during front door closure

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK DOOR CLOSURE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the handle in open position, simulate various closure/opening manoeuvres and check for noise and/or stiffness from the door detent link area. Check that the link is properly secured and lubricated.

Move on to Step

1. Link incorrectly secured

1. Renew the correct fastening

2. No lubricant

2. Lubricate with the specified grease

2

CHECK WITH DOOR OPEN

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Close door catch manually. Check catch fastening and for the presence of lubrication

Move on to Step

1. Catch incorrectly secured

1. Renew the correct fastening

2. Lack of lubricant

2. Lubricate with the specified grease

3

CHECK DOOR CLOSURE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the handle in open position, simulate various closure/opening manoeuvres and check for interference with the striker

Move on to Step

The striker is incorrectly adjusted

Adjust the striker

4

CHECK DOOR ALIGNMENT/SQUARENESS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the door against the vehicle body and the striker removed, check the door is correctly aligned. In particular, check the door openings and alignments with the front wing and sliding side door

Squareness is incorrect and the openings and alignments are not continuous.

Adjust the door hinges to restore door squareness and restore the correct openings and alignments. Refit the striker and adjust.
