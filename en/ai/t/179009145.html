Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

When reverse is engaged, a switch I020 (pin 1) closes its internal contact and sends an earth signal to engine compartment control unit B001 (pin 13 connector F).

On versions with automatic transmission, switch I020 is not present: the same function is performed by a contact "R" of automatic transmission solenoid unit L050 that is connected to pin 15 of connector F of B001 from pin A1.

Control unit B001 (pins 11 and 12 connector F) communicates reverse engagement via the CAN line to body computer M001 (pins 27 and 25 connector E respectively), which takes action to:

- turn on the reversing lights from pins 15 and 16 of connector H;

See E2022 REVERSING LIGHTS

- supply parking sensor M084 at pin 10 of connector A via a line protected by fuse F12 from pin 11 of connector F.

If a trailer is connected, body computer node M001 (pins 37 and 39 connector I) receive a connection signal from trailer service control unit and inhibit (pins 36 and 38 connector I) parking sensor M084 (pins 5 aned 14 connector A).

See E2050 TRAILER WIRING

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

Control unit M084 receives signals from parking sensor K097 at connector B. In particular:

- pins 2 of the sensors are connected to pins 9, 10, 3 and 4;

- pins 1 to pin 8;

- pins 3 to pin 2.

The reverse engagement and obstacle detection buzzer is built into the system.
