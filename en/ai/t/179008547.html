Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The vehicle is equipped with two reversing lights located in the rear light clusters.

The reversing lights are activated each time reverse gear is engaged: there is a switch on the outside of the gearbox casing.

The reversing lights circuit is supplied when the key is in position M.

The signal confirming engagement is managed by the Body Computer. The information is then forwarded to the steering column switch unit (steering wheel node control unit) which:

- if the windscreen wipers are working, activates the automatic operation of the rearscreen wiper;

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

- activates the electro-chrome rear view mirror;

See E3032 DOOR MIRROR ADJUSTMENT

- enables the parking sensor control unit.

See E3090 PARKING SENSOR
