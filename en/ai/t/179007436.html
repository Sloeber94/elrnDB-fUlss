1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Release the cover.

3. Partly extract the lower centre pillar trim element.

4. Disconnect the electrical connections.

- Place the lower centre pillar trim element at the side.

Removing

- Position the air duct in the pillar trim and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the electrical connections.

- Place the lower centre pillar trim element in position.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.

- Position the cover.

- Place the upper centre pillar trim element in position.

- Tighten the fixing bolt.

- Position the cover.
