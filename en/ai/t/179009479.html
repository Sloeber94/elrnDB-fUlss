Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

Air Bag control unit M060 receives power from two lines (pins 47 and 37) from body computer node M001 (pins 5 and 11 connector F respectively), of which only one (the first) is ignition-operated.

Body computer node M001 communicates with the various control units via the CAN line, in particular:

- from pins 23 and 21 of connector L, with Air Bag control unit M060 (pins 30 and 33 respectively);

- from pins 3 and 5 of connector G, with steering wheel node control unit M093 (pins 3 and 1 respectively);

- from pins 8 and 10 of connector D, with instrument panel E050 (pins 6 and 12) and warning light and odometer control unit M140 (pins 2 and 5).

Control units E050 (pin 13) and M140 (pin 4) receive power from body computer node M001 (pin 6 connector D) with one line protected by fuse F14.

Control unit M093 receives power (pin 5 connector A and pin 4 connector D) from body computer node M001 (pin 11 connector B and pin 1 connector G respectively) via two lines, of which only the second is protected by fuse F04.

Driver side airbag Q009 is connected to connector F of steering wheel node M093, which is in turn (pins 1-2 connectors B and C) is connected to airbag control unit M060 (pins 2-1, 20-21).

Switches I046 and I047 (pin 2) inform body computer node M001 (pins 37 and 39 connector L respectively) that the front seat belts are properly fastened.

Passenger side airbag Q011 (pins 1, 2, 3, 4 connector A) is connected to control unit M060 (pin 18, 19, 3, 4 respectively) and may be deactivated by means of switch H075 (pins 3 and 6) also connected to the same control unit (pins 49 and 50 respectively).

Control unit M060 is also connected:

- from pin 7 to pin 1 of connector A of passenger sidebag Q031;

- from pin 6 to pin 2 of passenger head bag Q041;

- from pin 11 to pin 2 of driver side head bag Q040;

- from pin 10 to pin 2 of driver side bag Q030;

- from pin 13 to pin 1 of driver side front belt pretensioner Q020;

- from pin 14 to pin 2 of driver side rear belt pretensioner Q022;

- from pin 23 to pin 2 of passenger side rear belt pretensioner Q023;

- from pin 22 to pin 1 of passenger side front belt pretensioner Q021;

- from pins 12 and 9 to pins 1 and 2 (respectively) of the sensor for driver side air bag K066;

- from pins 5 and 8 to pins 1 and 2 (respectively) of the sensor for passenger side air bag K067;
