Operational Description

The current socket P050 receives a supply from the body computer node M001 from 1 connector F through the line protected by fuse F07 of the body computer node M001.

F and from pin 7 connector H. The supply lines are

The lighting for the socket P050 - pin 2 - arrives from the line protected by fuse F12 and is operated by a relay, located in the body computer node M001.

The current socket P050 is earthed at pin 3.

Sockets P051 and P052 receive a supply from the lines protected by fuses F40 and F39, respectively, located in the additional fuse box B098

The sockets P051 and P052 are earthed at pin 3.

Sockets P051 and P052 are not lit up.
