Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The left and right direction indicators are activated by raising or lowering a special lever on the left of the steering column switch unit. The function is activated with the ignition key turned to ON.

The hazard warning lights (all the indicators working simultaneously) are activated by a button in the centre of the dashboard. An LED (at the back of the control button) also comes on synchronized with the flashing of the exterior lights. This function is always activated.

The engagement of the direction indicators/hazard warning lights and the frequency of the flashing (sent to all the nodes involved via the CAN) are managed by the body computer.

The direction indicator warning lights in the instrument panel are also turned on in this way.
