The engine starts normally, though possibly with difficulty, then suddenly stalls

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • no water, other fluids or dirt in the fuel filter
  • no water, other fluids or dirt in the fuel tank
  • that there are no obstructions in the exhaust system

Move on to Step

1. Water, other fluids or dirt in the fuel filter

1. Replace the filter element

2. Water, other fluids or dirt in the fuel tank

2. Clean tank

3. obstructions in the exhaust system

3. Replace the exhaust system damaged component

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

9

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check, as described < R1 > , that the pump supply voltage is equal to 12 volt and that the current absorbed is between 2 and 10 Ampere

See Test 1040AA CHECK ON THE SUPPLY VOLTAGE AND THE ABSORPTION CURRENT FOR THE SUBMERGED PUMP

Move on to Step

1. Voltage lower than 12 volts

1. Check the wiring at the connection leads

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

2. Current greater than 10 Amps: pump defective

2. Replace the fuel pump assembly

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GUAGE CONTROL - R + R

3. Current less than 2 Amps: Jet pump blocked

3. Change fuel pump assembly < R1 > and clean tank

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GUAGE CONTROL - R + R
