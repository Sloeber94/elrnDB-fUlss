Refitting

Removing

Position the multifunction switch on the gearbox casing and screw in the fixing bolts without tightening them and proceed with the adjustment.

Multifunction switch adjustment

Fit the control lever and check that the gear selector lever is in position "N".

1. Rotate the multifunciton switch until it is in contact with the bolt, then connect a multimeter to the terminals (A).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v, 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Adjust the angular position of the switch until the resistance - R = 0 [ohm] - mark the position of the switch in relation to the gearbox casing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v, 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Rotate the switch in a clockwise direction until the contact opens - R = infinity - mark the position of the switch in relation to the gearbox casing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v, 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Rotate the switch in an anti-clockwise direction adjusting the reference on the switch to an intermediate position in relation to the references on the gearbox casing.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v, 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Tighten the switch fixing bolts in this position to the recommended torque.
