Op. 5530B56 BATTERY DRIP TRAY COVER - R.R.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the rapid connector (1a) from the fuel manifold pipe and move the pipe (1b) aside.

2. Disconnect the electrical connections for the injectors.

3. Undo the fixing bolts (3a) and remove the single fuel manifold pipe complete with injectors (3b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the single fuel manifold pipe complete with injectors

- Tighten the bolts fixing the fuel manifold pipe.

- Connect the electrical connections for the injectors.

- Connect the rapid connector for the fuel supply pipe to the fuel manifold pipe.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
