N71 CONNECT DOES NOT RECEIVE VOICE MESSAGES

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When voice messages are given the corresponding functions are not activated.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The "voice command" function is managed by the telephone module which is integrated in the CONNECT whilst the microphone is located on the outside.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

All references to R/R or repair operations and wiring diagrams may be found in the Workshop Manual

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

- Enter the "Voice command configuration" page and then, in order:

"Sound configuration" - "Activate voice commands"

Check that they are activated (the button at the bottom left should show "Deactivated")

Deactivate it and the activate it straight away.

Move on to Step 1.

Settings not correct.

Restore correct values.

1

INTERNAL AMPLIFIER OPERATION CHEK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the activation of the steering column switch unit, using the Examiner, and that the telematic system is recognized by the Body Computer control unit.

Move on to Step 2.

System not recognized.

Replace the steering column switch unit.

Op. 5550A12 COMPLETE STALK UNIT - R+R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR BAG

or the connector

Op. 5580P20 CENTRALINA DEL CONNECT - S.R.

Italian market: contact TESEO

2

SPEAKER CONNECTION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the CONNECT to check the continuity/insulation of the connecting cable between the CONNECT and the microphone in the courtesy light.

See E4090 NAVIGATOR

Move on to Step 3.

Fault in the microphone wiring.

Restore the electrical connection.

3

AMPLIFIED AERIAL CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity/insulation of the microphone in the courtesy light.

Move on to Step 4.

Difficulties in recognizing the commands.

Repair or replace the microphone.

Op. 5580P20 CENTRALINA DEL CONNECT - S.R.

4

AERIAL CONDITION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Replace the CONNECT
