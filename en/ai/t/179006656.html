Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The alarm system comes on automatically the moment the doors are locked using the remote control.The remote control receiver is incorporated in the Steering Wheel Node and is fitted with an aerial for receiving the signal.The networks are on stand-by 30 mins after the engine is switched off. This makes it impossible to carry out the fault diagnosis. If a diagnostic session has to be opened, start up the engine to reawaken the networks.
