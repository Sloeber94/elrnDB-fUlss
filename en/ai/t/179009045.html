Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The front seats may be equipped with a special heating system comprising two elements located in the cushion and the backrest: this allows the seat to be heated to overcome the discomfort of cold passenger compartments, e.g. when the car is left in the open for long periods in winter.

The heating device, located in both front seats, consists of two resistors operated by a switch located on the outside of each seat.

There are four positions for the control for switching the heating system on/off:

- 0 = off;

- 1 = minimum heating;

- 2 = medium heating;

- 3 = maximum heating.

A special LED in the actual switches indicates that the resistances are switched on.

The resistances can be switched on with the engine running.
