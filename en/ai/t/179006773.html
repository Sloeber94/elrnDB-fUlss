Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Infiltration is noted through the sun roof (front, central and/or rear).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

OPERATIONS TO BE CARRIED OUT

TEST RESULTS

1

CHECK ROOF SEAL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Wtih the roof closed, check for profiling/deformation of perimeter seals. Ensure seals are properly compressed.

Move on to Step

The seals are damaged (deformed, unseated)

Replace seal

2

CHECK CORRECT ROOF OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check roof moves correctly

Move on to Step

Roof movement is not uniform (out of phase) and one side is not aligned with the opposite side when closed

Adjust timing (align slides)

3

CHECK FOR WATER INFILTRATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Carry out a water test to check for leaks from the sun roof

Move on to Step

A water infiltration is detected between the sun roof frame and the area where it fits to the roof

Replace / position the seal between the sun roof frame and roof

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present
