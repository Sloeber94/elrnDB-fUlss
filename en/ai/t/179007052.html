1060G10 FLOW METER - R R

Path
Procedures > 10 ENGINE > 1060 DIESEL INJECTION SYSTEM > 1060G DIESEL INJECTION PRESSURE PUMP ELECTRONIC CONTROL > 1060G10 FLOW METER - R R
Kind
repair procedure (removal / refitting; the operation number is the code)
Applies to
engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fUlss/en/#/179007052

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the air flow meter.

- Open the retaining band between the air flow meter and the turbo flexible air inlet pipe.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the air flow meter (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Connect the air flow meter to the air filter and fasten it using the bolts.

- Connect the air flow meter to the turbo flexible air inlet pipe and fit a new retaining band.

- Connect the electrical connection for the air flow meter.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Applies to: engines: 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods
