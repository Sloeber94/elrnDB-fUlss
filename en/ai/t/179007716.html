7020C80 SUN BLIND FOR LEFT OR RIGHT SLIDING SIDE DOOR S-R

Op. 7020C30 SLIDING DOOR INTERIOR PANEL - R+R

1. Remove the terminals on the sliding side door interior panel.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the three sealant strips that fasten the reinforcement frame to the interior panel.

2. Remove the reinforcement frame.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Release the blind from the anchorage clips on the frame.

1. Remove the sun blind.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the bind in its seat and secure to the frame with anchorage clips.

- Sand the areas on the reinforcement frame where sealant was present to ensure a better bond.

- Apply a layer of type E13 silicone filler along the base of the blind.

- Position the reinforcement frame in its seat and secure to the interior panel, applying E13 silicone filler at the points where the sealant strips were present.

- Fit the terminals on the sliding side door interior panel.

- Check that the sun blind is working.

- Carry out
