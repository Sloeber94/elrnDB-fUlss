1060F20 PIPE FROM PRESSURE PUMP TO FUEL MANIFOLD - R.R.

Kind

Applies to
engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Disconnect the three rapid connectors for the fuel pipes from the fuel filter.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.

3. Undo the bolts fixing the fuel filter to the mounting bracket.

4. Remove the fuel filter complete with filter element.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the retaining bolts.

2. Remove the fuel filter mounting.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

1. Undo the connector for the pipe from the return manifold and the connector for the pipe from the pressure pump.

2. Remove the pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Fit the air hose from the intake manifold to the air/air heat exchanger.

Fit the new pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold and tighten to torque.

PIPE FROM PRESSURE PUMP TO FUEL MANIFOLD - R.R.

Each time work is carried out on the pipe from the pressure pump to the fuel manifold it must be replaced with a new one to prevent fuel leaks from the connectors.

Tighten the connectors on the pressure pump and on the return manifold to torque.

Fit the fuel filter mounting and tighten the fixing bolts.

Tighten the bolts fixing the fuel filter to the mounting bracket.

Connect the electrical connection.
