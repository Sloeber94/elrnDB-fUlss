Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD, 2.2 16V JTD; all production periods; only with equipment: MY 2005

The command for the electrical heating of the front seats is activated by switches I065 and I066 housed in the actual seats.

The body computer node M001 supplies (pin 7 connector H) the switches I065 and I065 (pin 3) and the seat heating control units M110 and M170 (pin 3 connector A).

The switches I065 and I066 (pin 1) provide the control units M170 and M110 (pin 2 connector A) with a voltage that varies according to the position of the selector. Once the signal is received, the control units activate:

- from pins 1, 2, 3, 4 of connector B, the resistance for heating the cushion for the seat O046 and O047 (pins 1, 2, 3, 4 respectively);

- from pins 1 and 2 of connector C, the resistance for heating the backrest O048 and O049 (pins 1, 2).
