Op. 1020A20 CRANK CASE OIL SUMP - R + R CLEAN SEALS FOR SEALANT APPLICATION

1. Undo the bolts securing the front crankshaft oil seal cover.

2. Remove the front crankshaft oil seal cover from its seat.

Applies to: engines: 2.0 16V JTD; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Remove the oil sealing ring from its seat on the front cover.

Because the oil seal must always be replaced, it may be destroyed when it is removed.

Apply sealant to the front cover contact surface.

Fit the cover and tighten the bolts to the specifed torque. Pretighten all the bolts and then tighten to the final torque.
